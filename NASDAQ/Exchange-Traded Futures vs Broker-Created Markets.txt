# Exchange-Traded Futures vs Broker-Created Markets

====================================
Case 1: Exchange-Traded Futures (NQ)
====================================

NQ trades on the **CME (Chicago Mercantile Exchange)**.

There is one central exchange that creates the market.


              CME Exchange
                   │
      ┌────────────┼────────────┐
      ▼            ▼            ▼
     IBKR      NinjaTrader   AMP Futures
      ▼            ▼            ▼
    Charts       Charts       Charts


Everyone is ultimately looking at the same underlying market.

Therefore:

• The overall trend is the same.
• Highs and lows should be almost identical.
• Market structure is the same.
• Price distribution is the same.

Small differences can still exist because of:

• Chart settings.
• Session hours (Regular Trading Hours vs Extended Trading Hours).
• Candle aggregation method.
• Data latency.
• Missing or corrected ticks.

However, traders are generally observing the same market.

====================================
Case 2: Broker-Created Markets
====================================

Some financial products are created and priced by brokers.

Examples:

• CFDs.
• OTC Forex.
• Binary options.
• Synthetic indices.

The structure looks different:


	Broker A
		│
	Own Price Feed


	Broker B
		│
	Own Price Feed


	Broker C
		│
	Own Price Feed


Now prices can differ because each broker may:

• Derive prices differently.
• Apply its own spread.
• Smooth market data.
• Quote independently.

In these markets, charts may differ significantly.

====================================
Example: IQ Option
====================================

Remember the IQ Option API experiments.

You encountered instruments such as:

• EURUSD.
• EURUSD-OTC.
• Digital Options.
• CFDs.

These are not necessarily the same market.

Example:

```text
EURUSD

and

EURUSD-OTC
```

are different products.

EURUSD:

• Represents the traditional forex market.
• References the global currency exchange market.

EURUSD-OTC:

• Is created and priced by the broker.
• Often exists outside normal underlying market hours.
• Does not necessarily match centralized exchange data.

Therefore:

• You should not expect OTC charts to match centralized market charts exactly.
• Statistical analysis from one OTC feed may not transfer to another broker's OTC feed.

====================================
Why Databento + IBKR Makes Sense for NQ
====================================

For CME NQ futures:

• Databento provides CME market data.
• IBKR provides access to trade the CME futures contract.

Conceptually:


			  CME
			   │
		┌──────┴──────┐
		▼             ▼
	Databento       IBKR
		▼             ▼
	Python        TWS Chart


The underlying trades originate from CME.

Therefore:

• Your statistical models are analyzing the same market you are trading.
• Your Python research data and execution platform describe the same underlying price movement.
• Differences are mainly presentation, timing, and data handling.

====================================
Rule of Thumb: Who Creates the Price?
====================================

When evaluating a market, ask:

```text
Who creates the price?
```

If the answer is:

```text
Exchange

Examples:
CME
NYSE
NASDAQ
```

Then:

• Different brokers should look very similar.
• They are distributing the same underlying market data.
• Research is more portable across brokers.

If the answer is:

```text
Broker
```

Then:

• Charts can differ legitimately.
• Each broker may produce its own quotes.
• Statistical models may not transfer reliably.

====================================
Why Professionals Prefer Exchange-Traded Futures
====================================

Professional quantitative traders often prefer exchange-traded futures because:

• Everyone analyzes the same underlying market.
• Data is centralized.
• Price discovery happens in one location.
• Research results are more portable.

Example:

If a statistical model works on CME NQ data:

```text
CME NQ Data

↓

Statistical Model

↓

Trading Decision
```

The model should generally remain applicable whether execution happens through:

• IBKR.
• NinjaTrader Brokerage.
• AMP Futures.
• Other brokers connected to the same CME contract.
