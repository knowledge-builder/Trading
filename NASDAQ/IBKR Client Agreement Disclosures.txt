# Understanding the Key IBKR Client Agreement Disclosures

====================================
1. You Are Responsible for Your Trades
====================================

Once you click **Buy** or **Sell**, the trade becomes your responsibility.

This remains true even if:

* You made a mistake.
* You misunderstood the market.
* Your strategy failed.

In general, **IBKR is not responsible for your trading losses**.

---

====================================
2. Trading Involves Substantial Risk
====================================

IBKR makes it clear that:

* You can lose money.
* Markets can move rapidly.
* Past performance does not predict future results.

This is especially important for **futures**, where relatively small price movements can produce significant gains or losses because of leverage.

---

====================================
3. Orders Are Not Guaranteed
====================================

When you place an order:

* It may not execute.
* It may execute at a different price than expected (slippage).
* It may only partially fill.

**Example**

During a fast-moving market, a market order may execute at a different price than the last quote you saw.

---

====================================
4. Margin Requirements Can Change
====================================

If you're approved for futures trading, **IBKR may change margin requirements at any time**.

**Example**

```text
Today

Initial Margin = $20,000

↓

Tomorrow

Initial Margin = $24,000
```

If your account no longer satisfies the new requirement:

* You may need to deposit additional funds.
* IBKR may take action as permitted under the client agreement.

---

====================================
5. Electronic Systems Can Fail
====================================

You acknowledge that:

* Internet connections can fail.
* Exchanges may experience outages.
* Trading platforms may become unavailable.

These operational risks are part of electronic trading.

---

====================================
6. Market Data Is Not Guaranteed
====================================

Market quotes may be:

* Delayed
* Interrupted
* Unavailable
* Occasionally inaccurate

Many professional traders monitor multiple market data sources whenever possible.

---

====================================
7. You Are Responsible for Account Security
====================================

You are responsible for:

* Keeping your password secure.
* Protecting your two-factor authentication (2FA).
* Reporting suspected unauthorized access immediately.

---

====================================
8. Taxes Are Your Responsibility
====================================

Although IBKR provides tax reporting where required, you remain responsible for complying with your own country's tax laws.

For a Philippine resident, this includes understanding:

* Local Philippine tax obligations.
* Any applicable U.S. withholding rules.

---

====================================
9. Electronic Delivery
====================================

By accepting the agreement, you generally consent to receive documents electronically through the Client Portal, including:

* Account statements
* Trade confirmations
* Tax documents
* Official notices

instead of receiving paper mail.

---

====================================
10. IBKR May Restrict or Close Accounts
====================================

Under certain circumstances, IBKR may restrict or close an account, including situations involving:

* Regulatory requirements
* Suspected fraud
* Violations of the client agreement

---

====================================
11. Futures Are Different from Stocks
====================================

| Stocks | Futures |
|---------|----------|
| Buy company shares | Buy a standardized contract |
| PDT rule may apply to margin accounts | PDT rule does not apply |
| Stock margin rules | Futures margin rules |
| Own company shares | Hold a futures contract with defined obligations |

Understanding this distinction is important because margin, leverage, and risk operate differently in futures markets.

---

====================================
12. Your Trading Workflow
====================================


	Python
		│
		▼
	Research
		│
		▼
	Probability Model
		│
		▼
	Human Decision
		│
		▼
	IBKR
		│
		▼
	NQ Futures


Your workflow separates:

* Research
* Signal generation
* Human decision-making
* Trade execution

IBKR serves as your **execution platform**, while your analysis and probability models operate independently.

This separation helps reduce operational risk, but once you submit an order, **you are responsible for both the trading decision and its outcome**.

---

====================================
Three Clauses to Read Carefully
====================================

If you do not have time to read the entire agreement immediately, pay particular attention to these sections:

* **Client Responsibilities** — Your obligations as the account holder.
* **Futures & Margin Risk** — How futures margin works and the implications of changing margin requirements.
* **Liquidation Rights** — The circumstances under which IBKR may liquidate positions or take action on your account under the agreement.

These sections have the greatest practical impact for traders planning to manually trade futures through IBKR.
