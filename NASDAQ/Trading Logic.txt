# Trading Logic (Inferred)

====================================
1. Market Context Identification
====================================

The first step is mapping important reference levels.

These levels are not random indicators.

They are based on:

• Time-based reference points.
• Higher timeframe reference points.
• Previous market structure.
• Liquidity zones where traders may react.

Before entering a trade, he creates a map of important price zones.

Example:


	Resistance

	4095

	4081
	────── 4H Open


	4073
	────── New York Open


	4064
	────── Asia Low


	4050
	────── Previous 4H Low


	4041
	────── Entry Area


	4034
	────── NY/London Low


	4025
	────── Quarterly Open


	Support


The main question:

> "Where are important price zones where traders may react?"

====================================
3. Wait for Price to Reach a Key Level
====================================

He does not appear to chase price.

	The sequence:

	
	Before:

	Price:
	4080


	↓

	Falls


	↓

	4050


	↓

	4041
	

	He waits for price to reach a meaningful area.

	Example:

	
	Previous 4H Low

	+

	NY/London Low

	=

	Potential Reaction Zone
	

====================================
4. Look for Reaction
====================================

	The entry is not:


	Price touches level

	↓

	Buy
	

	Instead:


	Price reaches support

			↓

	Observe behavior

			↓

	Does selling pressure weaken?

			↓

	Does price reclaim level?

			↓

	Enter


Possible confirmations:

• Rejection candle.
• Failure to continue lower.
• Break back above the level.
• Momentum shift.
• Change in market behavior.

====================================
5. Execute Position
====================================

Example:

	
	BUY

	5 GC Contracts


	Entry:

	4041.5
	

The trading thesis:

> "This support area should hold, and price should rotate upward."

====================================
6. Manage the Trade
====================================

After entry:

### Scenario A — Wrong

	Price continues lower:

	
	4041

	↓

	4030

	↓

	Support fails
	

	Action:

	• Exit the position.
	• The original thesis is invalidated.


### Scenario B — Right

	Price moves upward:

	
	4041

	↓

	4050

	↓

	4065
	

	Action:

	• Take profit.
	• Move toward the next reference level.

====================================
7. Exit Logic
====================================

The exit appears to target another liquidity/reference level.

Example:


	Entry:

	4041.5


	↓

	Target:

	4065.5
	

	Why?

	Because around:

	
	4064
	

	was:

	
	Asia Low
	

	The logic:

	
	Entry

	↓

	Next important reference level
	

====================================
Complete Trading Flow
====================================


	1. Mark important levels

			↓

	2. Determine market location

			↓

	3. Wait for price to reach level

			↓

	4. Observe reaction

			↓

	5. Enter when probability improves

			↓

	6. Place risk below/above invalidation

			↓

	7. Target next liquidity/reference level


====================================
Core Concept
====================================

The system is not predicting every price movement.

It is identifying:

• Where price is located.
• Where other traders may react.
• When the probability of a reaction improves.
• Where the trade idea becomes invalid.
• Where the next objective target exists.
