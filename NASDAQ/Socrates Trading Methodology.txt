# Trading Strategy Documentation
# Key Level Reaction Trading System (KLRTS)
# Reverse-Engineered from Socrates Trading Methodology

====================================
1. Overview
====================================

## Strategy Name

Key Level Reaction Trading System (KLRTS)

## Core Idea

The strategy is based on the principle:

"Price tends to react when it reaches important historical reference areas because other market participants are also observing those areas."

The trader does not attempt to predict every market movement.

Instead, the strategy focuses on:

	• Identifying important price locations.
	• Waiting for price to reach those locations.
	• Observing market behavior at those locations.
	• Entering only when reaction confirms the trade idea.
	• Managing risk with predefined limits.


====================================
2. Trading Philosophy
====================================

## Market View

The market is viewed as:

A continuous auction between buyers and sellers.

Price movement represents:

	• Supply and demand imbalance.
	• Institutional activity.
	• Trader positioning.
	• Market sentiment.

Historical price areas matter because traders remember previous interactions.

====================================
7. Entry Logic
====================================

The strategy does not enter immediately when price reaches a level.


Incorrect:

	Price touches level

	↓

	Immediately buy/sell


	Correct:

	Price reaches key level

	↓

	Observe market behavior

	↓

	Determine if reaction occurs

	↓

	Confirm with volume/context

	↓

	Execute trade


====================================
8. Reaction Confirmation
====================================

A key level becomes a trade opportunity only when price behavior confirms it.


Possible confirmations:


	• Rejection

	Example:

	Price moves down

	↓

	Touches support

	↓

	Buyers reject lower prices

	↓

	Price closes higher


	• Failure to Continue

Example:

	Selling pressure

	↓

	Momentum decreases

	↓

	Price cannot break lower


	• Market Structure Shift

	Example:

	Lower lows

	↓

	Failure to make new low

	↓

	Break above previous short-term high


	• Volume Confirmation

	Volume is used as confirmation.

	Purpose:

	• Determine whether participation supports the move.

====================================

Scenario A — Trade Failure


	Example:

	Entry:

	4041.5


	↓

	Price breaks support

	4030


	Interpretation:

	• Original thesis is invalid.


	Action:

	• Exit trade.
	• Accept loss.
	• Move on.


Scenario B — Trade Success


	Example:

	Entry:

	4041.5


	↓

	4050


	↓

	4065


	Interpretation:

	• Price is moving toward the next reference area.


	Action:

	• Take profit.
	• Manage position toward target.

	↓

	↓

	Exit

12. Risk Management Framework
====================================

Risk management is part of the strategy.


====================================
12.1 Daily Objective
====================================

Before trading:

Define:

• Daily Profit Target.
• Maximum Acceptable Loss.


Example:

Profit Goal:

+$5,000


Maximum Loss:

-$4,000


====================================
12.2 Trade Frequency
====================================

Typical activity:

• 1 - 4 trades per day.


The trader does not need to trade every day.


====================================
12.3 Consecutive Loss Rule
====================================

Rule:

Two losing trades in a row

↓

Stop trading for the day


Purpose:

• Prevent revenge trading.
• Prevent emotional decisions.
• Prevent overtrading.


====================================
12.4 Account Protection
====================================

The trader uses automatic limits.


	Example:

	Maximum Daily Loss

	↓

	Account Lockout


	Purpose:

	• Prevent catastrophic decisions.
