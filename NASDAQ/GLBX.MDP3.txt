# GLBX.MDP3 — CME Futures Market Data Feed Documentation

====================================
1. Overview
====================================

GLBX.MDP3 represents the **CME Globex Market Data Platform 3.0**.

It provides access to:

• Real-time market data.
• Historical market data.
• Exchange-originated futures data.

For futures research, GLBX.MDP3 is important because it represents data originating from the actual exchange where futures contracts are traded.

====================================
2. Understanding the Name
====================================

## GLBX

GLBX = **Globex**

CME Globex is the electronic trading platform operated by CME Group.

It is where many major futures products are traded.

Examples:

| Market                | Products               |
|-----------------------|--++++++++++++++--------|
| Equity Index Futures  | NASDAQ-100, S&P 500    |
| Metals Futures        | Gold, Silver           |
| Energy Futures        | Crude Oil, Natural Gas |
| Agricultural Futures  | Corn, Wheat, Soybeans  |
| Interest Rate Futures | Treasury Futures       |

------------------------------------

## MDP3

MDP3 = **Market Data Platform 3.0**

It is CME's market data distribution system.

It publishes:

• Trades.
• Quotes.
• Order book information.
• Market updates.

4. GLBX.MDP3 vs Broker Platforms
====================================

A common misunderstanding:

> Brokers create the market data.

They do not.

The exchange creates the market.

Example:


                CME Globex

                    |

        ------------------------

        |          |           |

        ▼          ▼           ▼

     IBKR     Tradovate   NinjaTrader


        |

        ▼

    Trader Interface


All brokers access the same underlying exchange market.

Therefore:

• The source market is identical.
• Differences usually come from chart settings, data handling, or latency.

10. GLBX.MDP3 vs Synthetic Markets
====================================

## Exchange Data

	Example:


	GLBX.MDP3

	↓

	CME Futures


	Characteristics:

	• Real transactions.
	• Centralized exchange.
	• Regulated market.
	• Transparent pricing.
	• Shared market structure.

------------------------------------

## Synthetic / Broker-Created Markets

Examples:

• Some OTC products.
• Synthetic indices.
• Certain CFD feeds.

Characteristics:

• Broker may generate pricing.
• Not directly connected to an exchange.
• Different liquidity model.
• Different price formation process.

12. Core Principle
====================================

	GLBX.MDP3 is not a trading strategy.

	It is the measurement instrument.

	It provides access to the environment where professional futures traders operate.

	The objective:


	Observe the market accurately first.

	↓

	Develop understanding second.

	↓

	Consider execution only after evidence
	and risk controls exist.
