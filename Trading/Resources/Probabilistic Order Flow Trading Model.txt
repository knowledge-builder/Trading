============
Layer 1 — Mathematical Foundation (Highest Priority)
============

• 1. Probability Theory
  • Sample spaces
  • Random variables
  • Probability distributions
  • Joint probability
  • Conditional probability
  • Independence
  • Law of Total Probability
  • Bayes' Theorem
  • Expected value
  • Variance
  • Covariance
  • Core idea
    • Your model is fundamentally:
      • P(Win | Market State)
    • Without probability, the project doesn't exist.

• 2. Statistics
  • Sampling
  • Maximum Likelihood Estimation (MLE)
  • Confidence intervals
  • Hypothesis testing
  • Regression
  • Correlation
  • Bootstrap
  • Cross-validation
  • Goal
    • Determine whether your estimated edge is statistically meaningful.

• 3. Bayesian Statistics
  • Prior
  • Likelihood
  • Posterior
  • Bayesian updating
  • Conjugate priors
  • Sequential updating
  • Mindset
    • Instead of:
      • "The probability of winning is 62%."
    • Think:
      • "Given new order flow evidence, update my belief."
    • This closely matches how order flow behaves.

• 4. Linear Algebra
  • Vectors
  • Matrices
  • Dot product
  • Matrix multiplication
  • Eigenvalues (basic intuition)
  • Purpose
    • Understand the vector-based foundations of machine learning libraries.

• 5. Optimization
  • Cost functions
  • Gradient descent
  • Convex optimization (basic)
  • Regularization
  • Purpose
    • Machine learning is fundamentally an optimization problem.

• 6. Time Series
  • Autocorrelation
  • Lag variables
  • Rolling statistics
  • Stationarity
  • Regime changes
  • Purpose
    • Markets are sequential data.

• 7. Stochastic Processes
  • Markov chains
  • Random walks
  • Brownian motion
  • Poisson processes
  • Purpose
    • Useful for modeling transitions between market states.
