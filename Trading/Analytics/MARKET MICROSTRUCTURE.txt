===============================================================================
MARKET MICROSTRUCTURE
A Research-Oriented Learning Roadmap
===============================================================================

Objective
---------

Learn market microstructure as a researcher rather than as a
traditional finance student.

The goal is not to memorize financial terminology.

The goal is to understand the underlying mechanisms that generate
market behavior and eventually produce OHLCV data.

Every topic should answer the question:

    "Can this be measured, tested, and eventually used
     to improve market understanding?"

===============================================================================
Learning Philosophy
===============================================================================

Traditional Learning

    Learn Theory

            ↓

    Memorize Concepts

            ↓

    Take Examination

Research-Oriented Learning

    Learn Theory

            ↓

    Understand Mechanism

            ↓

    Observe Data

            ↓

    Form Hypothesis

            ↓

    Test with Python

            ↓

    Measure Results

            ↓

    Improve Understanding

The objective is not knowledge alone.

The objective is evidence-based understanding.

===============================================================================
Week 1
Order Book Fundamentals
===============================================================================

Learning Objective

    Understand exactly what the order book represents and how it
    reflects current market intentions.

Topics

    • Bid Orders

    • Ask Orders

    • Price Levels

    • Quantity

    • Market Depth

    • Best Bid

    • Best Ask

    • Spread

Questions

    □ What does each field represent?

    □ What information does each value provide?

    □ Which values change most frequently?

    □ How does the order book evolve over time?

    □ What events cause updates?

Expected Outcome

    Build a solid understanding of the current state of market
    supply and demand.

===============================================================================
Week 2
Trade Execution
===============================================================================

Learning Objective

    Understand how orders interact to produce completed trades.

Topics

    • Market Orders

    • Limit Orders

    • Trade Matching

    • Executions

    • Aggressive Buyers

    • Aggressive Sellers

Questions

    □ How are orders matched?

    □ What creates an execution?

    □ What is the difference between market orders and limit orders?

    □ Who initiates the trade?

    □ What information is lost once a trade is completed?

Expected Outcome

    Understand how market participants transform intentions
    (orders) into actual transactions.

===============================================================================
Week 3
Liquidity
===============================================================================

Learning Objective

    Understand how liquidity influences short-term price movement.

Topics

    • Liquidity

    • Liquidity Walls

    • Order Cancellation

    • Order Addition

    • Liquidity Consumption

Questions

    □ Why does liquidity appear?

    □ Why does liquidity disappear?

    □ Why are liquidity walls created?

    □ Why are liquidity walls cancelled?

    □ How does changing liquidity affect price movement?

Expected Outcome

    Develop intuition about how liquidity constrains or enables
    short-term market movement.

===============================================================================
Week 4
Price Formation
===============================================================================

Learning Objective

    Understand how market activity evolves into observable price data.

Learning Flow

        Order Book

                ↓

        Order Flow

                ↓

        Trade Executions

                ↓

        Liquidity Changes

                ↓

        Price Movement

                ↓

        OHLCV

Questions

    □ How does order flow influence price?

    □ Why does price accelerate?

    □ Why does price reverse?

    □ What creates trends?

    □ What creates volatility?

    □ How are OHLCV candles formed from market activity?

Expected Outcome

    Understand that OHLCV is not the starting point of analysis,
    but rather the observable outcome of interactions between
    market participants.

===============================================================================
Research Mindset
===============================================================================

For every topic studied, continuously ask

    □ Can this phenomenon be measured?

    □ Can historical data be collected?

    □ Can a hypothesis be formulated?

    □ Can the hypothesis be tested statistically?

    □ Can the findings improve market understanding?

Knowledge should naturally progress into research.

===============================================================================
Final Principle
===============================================================================

The purpose of studying market microstructure is not to become
an expert in financial terminology.

The purpose is to understand the underlying mechanisms that produce

    • Order Flow

    • Liquidity Changes

    • Price Movement

    • Trends

    • Reversals

    • Volatility

Only after understanding these mechanisms should statistical analysis,
hypothesis testing, and signal generation be developed.

The objective is not simply to predict price.

The objective is to understand the processes that generate price.
