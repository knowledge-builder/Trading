===============================================================================
THE RESEARCH MINDSET OF A FULL-TIME TRADER
===============================================================================

Objective
---------

Develop the knowledge, skills, and mindset required to continuously
research, validate, and adapt trading strategies as financial markets
evolve.

The objective is NOT to:

    • Find a strategy that works forever.

    • Predict every market movement.

    • Eliminate uncertainty.

The objective IS to:

    • Develop scientific thinking.

    • Think probabilistically.

    • Make evidence-based decisions.

    • Continuously research market behavior.

    • Adapt as market conditions change.

===============================================================================
CORE PRINCIPLE
===============================================================================

Do not become attached to a strategy.

Become attached to the research process.

Remember:

    • Markets evolve.

    • Participants evolve.

    • Technology evolves.

    • Regulations evolve.

    • Economic conditions evolve.

As markets evolve:

    • Old strategies may weaken.

    • Existing edges may disappear.

    • New opportunities may emerge.

Your competitive advantage should be:

    • The ability to discover new edges.

    • The ability to validate those edges.

    • The ability to recognize when they no longer exist.

===============================================================================
THE SCIENTIFIC RESEARCH PROCESS
===============================================================================

Step 1

    Observe the market.

        • Watch price behavior.

        • Study volume.

        • Analyze liquidity.

        • Examine order flow.

        • Monitor market structure.

Step 2

    Generate hypotheses.

        Example:

            "Large buy imbalances increase the probability of short-term
             upward price movement."

Step 3

    Collect market data.

        • Historical OHLCV

        • Tick Data

        • Order Book

        • Trades

        • Economic Events

Step 4

    Perform statistical analysis.

        • Descriptive Statistics

        • Probability Analysis

        • Hypothesis Testing

        • Correlation Analysis

        • Regression

        • Feature Evaluation

Step 5

    Validate findings.

        • In-Sample Testing

        • Out-of-Sample Testing

        • Walk-Forward Analysis

        • Cross Validation

Step 6

    Deploy cautiously.

        • Small position sizes.

        • Controlled risk.

        • Continuous monitoring.

Step 7

    Monitor performance.

        • Win Rate

        • Expectancy

        • Drawdown

        • Sharpe Ratio

        • Profit Factor

Step 8

    Detect changes.

        Ask:

            • Has volatility changed?

            • Has liquidity changed?

            • Has market participation changed?

            • Has the market entered a new regime?

            • Has my statistical edge weakened?

Step 9

    Update research.

        • Improve the hypothesis.

        • Modify the model.

        • Replace obsolete strategies.

        • Continue researching.

===============================================================================
QUESTIONS A RESEARCHER ASKS
===============================================================================

Instead of asking:

    "Why isn't my strategy working?"

Ask:

    • What assumptions did my strategy depend on?

    • Are those assumptions still valid?

    • Has the data distribution changed?

    • Has volatility increased?

    • Has liquidity decreased?

    • Has the spread widened?

    • Has order flow changed?

    • Is the observed performance statistically significant?

    • Is this random variation or structural change?
