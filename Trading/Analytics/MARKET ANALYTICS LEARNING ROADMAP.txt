===============================================================================
MARKET ANALYTICS LEARNING ROADMAP
===============================================================================

Objective
---------

Develop a strong understanding of how financial markets operate before
building statistical models and analytical software.

The objective is NOT simply to learn trading.

The objective is to understand:

    • How markets are structured.

    • How financial instruments work.

    • How prices are formed.

    • How liquidity affects market behavior.

    • How macroeconomic forces influence markets.

This roadmap serves as the domain knowledge foundation that complements
mathematics, statistics, programming, and market research.

===============================================================================
PHASE 1 — FINANCIAL INSTRUMENTS
===============================================================================

Objective

    Understand the products being analyzed.

Learning Focus

    • Stocks

    • Bonds

    • Futures Contracts

    • Options

    • ETFs

    • Swaps

    • Margin

    • Leverage

    • Settlement

Expected Outcome

    Develop a solid understanding of the financial instruments that
    generate market data.

===============================================================================
PHASE 2 — CAPITAL MARKETS
===============================================================================

Objective

    Learn how financial markets are organized.

Learning Focus

    • Primary Market

    • Secondary Market

    • Stock Exchanges

    • Futures Exchanges

    • Clearing Houses

    • Institutional Participants

    • Retail Participants

Expected Outcome

    Understand where transactions occur, who participates, and how
    financial assets move through the market ecosystem.

===============================================================================
PHASE 3 — MARKET MICROSTRUCTURE
===============================================================================

Objective

    Understand how prices are formed through the interaction of buyers
    and sellers.

Learning Focus

    • Limit Orders

    • Market Orders

    • Bid and Ask

    • Order Book

    • Market Makers

    • Price Discovery

    • Order Flow

    • Slippage

    • Spread

Expected Outcome

    Develop the ability to interpret order book data, execution quality,
    and short-term market behavior.

===============================================================================
PHASE 4 — LIQUIDITY
===============================================================================

Objective

    Understand how easily assets can be traded and how liquidity impacts
    execution and price movement.

Learning Focus

    • Market Depth

    • Trading Volume

    • Liquidity Providers

    • Liquidity Consumption

    • Impact Cost

    • Execution Quality

Expected Outcome

    Develop intuition about why identical strategies can produce
    different results under different liquidity conditions.

===============================================================================
PHASE 5 — COMMODITY MARKETS
===============================================================================

Objective

    Understand the structure of futures markets.

Learning Focus

    • Commodity Futures

    • Contract Specifications

    • Tick Size

    • Contract Expiration

    • Rolling Contracts

    • Contango

    • Backwardation

Expected Outcome

    Understand how futures contracts behave and how they differ from
    traditional equity markets.

===============================================================================
PHASE 6 — INTEREST RATES
===============================================================================

Objective

    Understand how interest rates influence financial markets.

Learning Focus

    • Central Banks

    • Monetary Policy

    • Yield Curves

    • Inflation

    • Discount Rates

    • Cost of Capital

Expected Outcome

    Develop an understanding of why macroeconomic events affect market
    prices and investor behavior.

===============================================================================
FINAL PRINCIPLE
===============================================================================

The objective of this roadmap is not to become a trader.

It is to understand how markets function before attempting to model,
analyze, or predict their behavior.

Understanding the market domain enables better:

    • Feature Engineering

    • Hypothesis Generation

    • Statistical Modeling

    • Market Research

    • Software Design

Ultimately,

    Market Knowledge

            ↓

    Better Research Questions

            ↓

    Better Mathematical Models

            ↓

    Better Software

            ↓

    Better Market Analytics

A strong quantitative researcher combines domain knowledge,
mathematics, statistics, and software engineering into a single
evidence-based research process.
