This is the First Mechanism of Price Movement

Market Liquidity: Refers to the ease of buying or selling an asset in a market without causing a drastic change in its price. 
For example, highly traded stocks (like Apple) have high market liquidity, while penny stocks often lack it

Price moves because available liquidity is consumed.

	Buy Market Order

	       ↓

	Consumes Sell Orders

	       ↓

	Next Seller is More Expensive

	       ↓

	Price Rises

	or

	Sell Market Order

	       ↓

	Consumes Buy Orders

	       ↓

	Next Buyer Pays Less

	       ↓

	Price Falls
