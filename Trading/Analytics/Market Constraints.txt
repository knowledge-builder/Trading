Market Constraints (Mechanisms to Explore)

1. Matching Constraint
	• Every trade requires:
		• Buyer
		• Seller

Question

	• How do buyers and sellers become matched?

===================================================
	
2. Liquidity Constraint
	• Price cannot move without available liquidity.

Question

	• How does liquidity enable or resist price movement?

===================================================

3. Order Interaction Constraint

	• Orders interact before price changes.

Question

	• How do market orders interact with limit orders?
	
===================================================
	
4. Order Book Constraint

	• Liquidity is finite at every price level.

Question

	• What happens when available liquidity is exhausted?

===================================================

5. Price Impact Constraint

	• Not every unit of volume moves price equally.

Question

	• Why does identical volume sometimes move price a lot, and other times almost not at all?

===================================================

6. Absorption Constraint

	• Opposing orders can absorb incoming pressure.

Question

	• How much buying pressure is required to absorb selling pressure?

===================================================
	
7. Momentum Constraint

	• Price movement tends to persist until something changes.

Question

	• What mechanisms terminate momentum?

===================================================
	
8. Reversal Constraint

	• Reversals are processes, not events.

Question

	• What conditions must exist before a reversal becomes possible?

===================================================
	
9. State Transition Constraint

	• The market changes from one state to another.

Question

	• Which state transitions are common?
	• Which are rare?
	• Which are impossible under normal market mechanics?

===================================================
	
10. Time Constraint

	• Markets evolve through time.

Question

	• How does information accumulate across 5m, 15m, 30m, and higher timeframes?

===================================================
	
Physics-Inspired Research Questions

Instead of asking:

What indicator predicts price?

Ask:

	• What interactions generate movement?
	• What variables constrain movement?
	• What variables store pressure?
	• What releases pressure?
	• What creates resistance?
	• What creates acceleration?
	• What creates equilibrium?
	• What breaks equilibrium?
	• Which market states are reachable?
	• Which transitions are impossible?
