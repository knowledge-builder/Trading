
================================================================================
LIQUIDITY AS CAPACITY
A Physics-Inspired Mental Model of Price Movement
================================================================================

Objective
---------
This document is NOT claiming that financial markets obey the laws of physics.

Instead, it borrows a scientific way of thinking:

    • Understand the mechanism first.
    • Measure it second.
    • Validate it statistically later.

The objective is to understand WHY price moves
before asking HOW OFTEN it moves.

================================================================================
BIG PICTURE
================================================================================

Forget:

    • Candlestick charts
    • Indicators
    • Technical analysis

Instead, imagine:

    • Buyers
    • Sellers
    • Orders
    • A Matching Engine

At its core, a market is simply a system that continuously
matches buyers and sellers.

================================================================================
PRICE LEVELS
================================================================================

Imagine each price level as a floor in a building.

                 Price = $102
             ─────────────────

                 Price = $101
             ─────────────────

                 Price = $100

Each floor contains people waiting to trade.

Example

Price = $100

Sell Orders

████████████████████

100 Contracts

Interpretation

    • Sellers are willing to sell
      a total of 100 contracts.

    • Those contracts are available
      specifically at $100.

================================================================================
THE KEY IDEA
================================================================================

Do NOT think of those 100 contracts as:

    • Mass
    • Force
    • Energy

Instead think of them as

    CAPACITY

Definition

Capacity is:

    • The amount of order flow
      that a price level can absorb
      before no liquidity remains.

================================================================================
SMALL BUY ORDER
================================================================================

Buyer submits

    • Buy 5 Contracts

Before

    • Capacity = 100 Contracts

After

    • Capacity = 95 Contracts

Observation

    • Plenty of liquidity remains.
    • The system absorbs the order.
    • Price usually does not change.

================================================================================
ANOTHER BUYER
================================================================================

Buyer submits

    • Buy 10 Contracts

Remaining Capacity

    • 95
      ↓
      85 Contracts

Observation

    • Liquidity still exists.
    • The current price continues
      absorbing buying pressure.

================================================================================
SPONGE ANALOGY
================================================================================

Imagine pouring water onto a sponge.

Water

↓↓↓↓↓

██████████████

Sponge

Initially

    • Water is absorbed.
    • Nothing spills.

Eventually

    • Sponge reaches capacity.
    • Additional water overflows.

Liquidity behaves similarly.

		Incoming Orders

				↓

		Resting Liquidity

				↓

		Absorption

				↓

		Capacity Exhausted

================================================================================
LARGE BUY ORDER
================================================================================

Institution submits

    • Buy 200 Contracts

Current Liquidity

    • 100 Contracts

Exchange executes

    • 100 Filled
    • 100 Remaining

Result

    • No liquidity remains at $100.

================================================================================
WHAT HAPPENS NEXT?
================================================================================

The exchange CANNOT invent sellers.

Instead it must:

    • Search for the next seller.
    • Continue matching orders.
    • Execute at the next available price.

Example

Price = $101

Available

    • 150 Contracts

Remaining order is completed here.

================================================================================
WHY DID PRICE RISE?
================================================================================

Price did NOT increase because

    • The exchange decided.
    • Buyers became stronger.
    • Sellers became weaker.

Price increased because

    • Liquidity at $100 was exhausted.
    • The next available liquidity
      existed at $101.

Price movement is therefore

    A consequence of liquidity exhaustion.

================================================================================
THE CORE INSIGHT
================================================================================

	Do NOT think

		Buying Volume
			  ↓
		Price Movement

	Instead think

		Buying Volume
				↓
	 Interacts With Liquidity
				↓
	 Liquidity Absorbs Orders
				↓
	 Capacity Exhausted
				↓
	 Transition To Next Price

The interaction is the mechanism.

Price movement is the consequence.

================================================================================
RESEARCH QUESTIONS
================================================================================

Instead of asking

    "Does buying volume move price?"

Ask

    • How much liquidity exists
      at each price level?

    • How quickly is liquidity consumed?

    • How quickly is liquidity replenished?

    • What determines the capacity
      of a price level?

    • How much buying pressure
      is required to exhaust liquidity?

    • Can liquidity capacity
      be estimated mathematically?

    • Why do some price levels
      absorb enormous volume,
      while others collapse almost immediately?

================================================================================
MENTAL MODEL
================================================================================

Price movement is NOT simply caused by:

    • Buyers
    • Sellers
    • Volume

Instead it emerges from the interaction between:

    • Incoming Order Flow
    • Available Liquidity
    • The Matching Engine

Liquidity continuously absorbs incoming orders.

Once its capacity is exhausted,

the exchange naturally continues matching
orders at the next available price level.

Price movement is therefore

    An emergent consequence
    of repeated interactions
    between order flow and liquidity.
