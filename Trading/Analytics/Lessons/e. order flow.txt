===============================================================================
ORDER FLOW
Core Concepts
===============================================================================

Definition
----------

Order Flow is the real-time observation of executed buy and sell trades.

It focuses on how aggressive market orders interact with resting limit
orders to produce price movement.

Unlike candlesticks, which summarize price, order flow explains how
those prices were formed.

===============================================================================
1. MARKET ORDERS
===============================================================================

Definition

    A market order is an aggressive order that executes immediately at
    the best available price.

Priority

    Speed over Price

Characteristics

    • Executes immediately.

    • Removes liquidity from the order book.

    • Can move price.

==================
Aggressive Buying
==================

Illustration

Current Order Book

    SELLERS (Ask)

    $100.00     10

    $100.05     20

    $100.10     30

--------------------------------------------

    BUYERS (Bid)

    $99.95      15

    $99.90      25

Market Buy Order

    Buy 25 Shares

Execution

    10 @ $100.00

    15 @ $100.05

Result

    Market Buy

            ↓

    Consumes Ask Liquidity

            ↓

    Price Moves Higher

Example

    Imagine entering a grocery store and saying:

        "I don't care about the price.

         Give me 25 apples immediately."

    You purchase whatever inventory is available.

Mental Model

    "Trade NOW."
	
===================
Aggressive Selling
===================

Definition

    A market sell order is an aggressive order that executes immediately
    at the best available buying prices.

Priority

    Speed over Price

Characteristics

    • Executes immediately.

    • Removes liquidity from the order book.

    • Can move price downward.

Illustration

Current Order Book

    SELLERS (Ask)

    $100.05     20

    $100.10     30

--------------------------------------------

    BUYERS (Bid)

    $100.00     10

    $99.95      15

    $99.90      25

Market Sell Order

    Sell 25 Shares

Execution

    10 @ $100.00

    15 @ $99.95

Result

    Market Sell

            ↓

    Consumes Bid Liquidity

            ↓

    Best Bid Disappears

            ↓

    Price Moves Lower

Example

    Imagine you urgently need cash.

    You walk into a marketplace and say:

        "I don't care what price I get.

         Buy all 25 apples from me immediately."

    Buyers purchase your apples beginning with the
    highest price they are willing to pay.

    Once those buyers are exhausted,

    your remaining apples are sold to buyers offering
    lower prices.

Mental Model

    "Sell NOW."

===============================================================================
2. LIMIT ORDERS
===============================================================================

Definition

    A limit order is a passive order that executes only at a specified
    price or better.

Priority

    Price over Speed

Characteristics

    • Waits in the order book.

    • Provides liquidity.

    • Does not immediately move price.

Illustration

Limit Sell Order

    Sell 100 Shares

    @ $100.00

Order Book

    SELLERS

    $100.00     110

    $100.05      20

Result

    Limit Order

            ↓

    Adds Liquidity

            ↓

    Waits for a Market Order

Example

    Selling your car for $15,000.

    You post the asking price and wait for someone willing to pay it.

Mental Model

    "Trade only at MY price."

===============================================================================
MARKET ORDER vs LIMIT ORDER
===============================================================================

Market Order

    • Aggressive

    • Immediate execution

    • Removes liquidity

    • May move price

------------------------------------------------------------

Limit Order

    • Passive

    • Waits for execution

    • Provides liquidity

    • Usually does not move price immediately

===============================================================================
3. DELTA
===============================================================================

Definition

    Delta is the difference between aggressive buying volume and
    aggressive selling volume.

Formula

    Delta

        =

    Aggressive Buy Volume

        -

    Aggressive Sell Volume

Example 1

    Aggressive Buying

        400 Shares

    Aggressive Selling

        250 Shares

    Delta

        400 - 250

        = +150

Interpretation

    Positive Delta

        More aggressive buying than selling.

------------------------------------------------------------

Example 2

    Aggressive Buying

        120 Shares

    Aggressive Selling

        350 Shares

    Delta

        120 - 350

        = -230

Interpretation

    Negative Delta

        More aggressive selling than buying.

Mental Model

    Delta answers:

        "Who was more aggressive?"

Important

    Delta does NOT answer:

        "Who won?"

===============================================================================
4. ABSORPTION
===============================================================================

Definition

    Absorption occurs when heavy aggressive buying or selling fails to
    move price because large resting limit orders absorb the incoming
    market orders.

Illustration

Aggressive Buyers

    BUY

    BUY

    BUY

    BUY

    BUY

Delta

    +5,000

Expected

    Price rises sharply.

Actual

    Price remains around $100.

Why?

    Large Limit Seller

    50,000 Shares

    @ $100

Every market buy is filled.

Price barely moves.

Result

    Aggressive Buying

            ↓

    Large Limit Seller

            ↓

    Orders Absorbed

            ↓

    Little or No Price Movement

Example

    Imagine filling a swimming pool.

    Every second:

        +10 liters enter.

    But a hidden drain removes:

        -10 liters.

    Water level stays the same.

    This is absorption.

Mental Model

    "Aggression failed to move price."

===============================================================================
SUMMARY
===============================================================================

Market Order

    • Aggressive

    • Executes immediately

    • Removes liquidity

    • Can move price

------------------------------------------------------------

Limit Order

    • Passive

    • Waits for execution

    • Provides liquidity

    • Usually does not move price immediately

------------------------------------------------------------

Delta

    • Measures aggressive buying vs. selling

    • Indicates which side was more aggressive

    • Does NOT indicate who won

------------------------------------------------------------

Absorption

    • Large limit orders absorb aggressive orders

    • High buying or selling activity

    • Little or no price movement

    • May indicate exhaustion and possible reversal

===============================================================================
THE ORDER FLOW PROCESS
===============================================================================

        Limit Orders
     (Resting Liquidity)

              ▲`
              │

      Market Orders
   (Aggressive Traders)

              │
              ▼

      Executed Trades

              │
              ▼

            Delta

              │
              ▼

    If Liquidity Runs Out

        Price Moves

              OR

    If Orders Are Absorbed

      Price Stalls

              │
              ▼

       OHLC Recorded

              │
              ▼

       Candlestick Drawn

===============================================================================
KEY TAKEAWAY
===============================================================================

Order Flow explains how prices are formed.

Market Orders create trades.

Limit Orders provide liquidity.

Delta measures aggression.

Absorption reveals when aggression fails to move price.

Only after these interactions occur are the Open, High, Low, and Close
(OHLC) prices recorded, producing the candlestick.
