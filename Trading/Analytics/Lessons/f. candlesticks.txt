===============================================================================
CANDLESTICKS
Price Summary, NOT Market Intent
===============================================================================

Definition
----------

A candlestick is a visual summary of price movement over a specific time
period.

It records only four prices:

    • Open

    • High

    • Low

    • Close

A candlestick does NOT explain how those prices were produced.

===============================================================================
WHAT A CANDLE TELLS YOU
===============================================================================

A candlestick answers:

    • Where did price open?

    • What was the highest traded price?

    • What was the lowest traded price?

    • Where did price close?

Example

    Open  = 100

    High  = 105

    Low   = 99

    Close = 104

Result

    Green Candle

Because

    Close > Open

===============================================================================
WHAT A CANDLE DOES NOT TELL YOU
===============================================================================

A candlestick alone cannot answer:

    • Who initiated the trades?

    • Were buyers aggressive?

    • Were sellers aggressive?

    • Was buying absorbed?

    • Was selling absorbed?

    • Was Delta positive or negative?

    • Was liquidity increasing or decreasing?

    • Why did price move?

===============================================================================
IMPORTANT DISTINCTION
===============================================================================

A green candle DOES NOT mean:

    • Buyers dominated.

    • Sellers were weak.

A red candle DOES NOT mean:

    • Sellers dominated.

    • Buyers were weak.

Candle color only compares:

    Close

        vs

    Open

Nothing more.

===============================================================================
THE PRICE FORMATION PROCESS
===============================================================================

Order Flow

        ↓

Executed Trades

        ↓

Price Changes

        ↓

Open, High, Low, Close (OHLC)

        ↓

Candlestick

A candlestick is the RESULT of trading activity.

It is NOT the trading activity itself.

===============================================================================
MENTAL MODEL
===============================================================================

Think of a candlestick as a summary report.

It tells you:

    "What happened."

It does NOT tell you:

    "How it happened."

To understand how price was formed, additional information is required,
such as:

    • Order Flow

    • Order Book

    • Delta

    • Liquidity

    • Market Orders

    • Limit Orders

===============================================================================
KEY TAKEAWAY
===============================================================================

Candlesticks describe the outcome of trading.

Market Microstructure explains the process that created that outcome.

Do not interpret candle color as evidence of buyer or seller dominance.

Instead:

    Candlestick

        → What happened?

    Order Flow

        → How did it happen?

    Market Microstructure

        → Why did it happen?


===============================================================================
GREEN CANDLE
===============================================================================

A green candle means:

    Close > Open

Illustration

        High
         │
         │
Close ├───┤
      │███│
      │███│
      │███│
Open  ├───┤
         │
         │
        Low

Example

    Open  = 100

    High  = 105

    Low   = 99

    Close = 104

Interpretation

    Price closed HIGHER than it opened.

It DOES NOT mean:

    • Buyers dominated.

    • Sellers were weak.

    • Buying pressure exceeded selling pressure.

===============================================================================
RED CANDLE
===============================================================================

A red candle means:

    Close < Open

Illustration

        High
         │
         │
Open ┌───┐
     │███│
     │███│
     │███│
Close├───┤
     └───┘
         │
         │
         ▼
         Low

Example

    Open  = 104

    High  = 106

    Low   = 98

    Close = 100

Interpretation

    Price closed LOWER than it opened.

It DOES NOT mean:

    • Sellers dominated.

    • Buyers were weak.

    • Selling pressure exceeded buying pressure.

===============================================================================
IMPORTANT PRINCIPLE
===============================================================================

A candle only compares:

        Open

            vs

        Close

Green Candle

    Close > Open

Red Candle

    Close < Open

Candle color is simply a visual representation of the relationship
between the Open and Close prices.

It is NOT evidence of order flow, buyer dominance, or seller dominance.
