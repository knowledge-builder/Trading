===============================================================================
FACTORS THAT INFLUENCE MARKET PRICES
===============================================================================

Market Microstructure recognizes that an asset's observed market price
is influenced not only by its fundamental value, but also by the trading
process itself.

These influences are commonly referred to as market frictions.

===============================================================================
MARKET FRICTIONS
===============================================================================

| Factor                    | Definition                      | Illustration                                | Example                                      |
|---------------------------|---------------------------------|---------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------|
| **Bid-Ask Spread**        | Bid vs. Ask price difference.   | Bid = $99.95, Ask = $100.00                 | Buying pays Ask; selling receives Bid.       |
| **Trading Costs**         | Costs of executing a trade.     | Price + Fees + Taxes + Spread               | Buying BTC costs more than the quoted price. |
| **Liquidity Constraints** | Limited buyers or sellers.      | Large order consumes available liquidity.   | Low-volume coins move sharply on big trades. |
| **Information Asymmetry** | Unequal access to information.  | Some traders know or react sooner.          | HFT reacts to news before retail traders.    |
| **Order Flow**            | Stream of buy and sell orders.  | Heavy buying pushes prices upward.          | Strong buying after positive news.           |
| **Market Participants**   | Entities trading in the market. | Retail, institutions, banks, market makers. | Pension fund orders move markets more.       |
| **Exchange Rules**        | Rules governing trading.        | Tick size, order types, trading hours.      | Circuit breakers pause trading.              |
| **Trading Technology**    | Technology used for execution.  | Faster systems execute sooner.              | HFT uses ultra-low-latency networks.         | 

===============================================================================
HOW EACH FACTOR INFLUENCES PRICE
===============================================================================

Bid-Ask Spread

    ↓

Different buying and selling prices increase transaction costs.

------------------------------------------------------------

Trading Costs

    ↓

Higher costs reduce trading efficiency and affect profitability.

------------------------------------------------------------

Liquidity Constraints

    ↓

Large orders may significantly move prices when insufficient liquidity
is available.

------------------------------------------------------------

Information Asymmetry

    ↓

Participants with faster or better information may react before others,
causing prices to adjust.

------------------------------------------------------------

Order Flow

    ↓

An imbalance between buy and sell orders influences short-term price
movement.

------------------------------------------------------------

Market Participants

    ↓

Different participants have different objectives, resources, and trading
behavior, all of which influence market dynamics.

------------------------------------------------------------

Exchange Rules

    ↓

Market structure and exchange regulations determine how orders are
processed and how prices evolve.

------------------------------------------------------------

Trading Technology

    ↓

Execution speed and trading infrastructure influence market efficiency
and price discovery.

===============================================================================
KEY INSIGHT
===============================================================================

Traditional Asset Pricing assumes:

    Fundamental Value

            ↓

        Market Price

------------------------------------------------------------

Market Microstructure recognizes that the observed market price is also
influenced by the mechanics of trading.

    Fundamental Value

            +

    Bid-Ask Spread

            +

    Trading Costs

            +

    Liquidity Constraints

            +

    Information Asymmetry

            +

    Order Flow

            +

    Market Participants

            +

    Exchange Rules

            +

    Trading Technology

            ↓

    Observed Market Price

===============================================================================
FINAL PRINCIPLE
===============================================================================

Market prices are not determined solely by an asset's intrinsic value.

The observed market price is the result of:

    • The asset's fundamental value.

    • The interaction of buyers and sellers.

    • The availability of liquidity.

    • The flow of market orders.

    • The structure of the exchange.

    • The technology used to execute trades.

Understanding these market frictions is the primary objective of
Market Microstructure.
