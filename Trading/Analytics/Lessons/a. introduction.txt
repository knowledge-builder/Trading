===============================================================================
MARKET MICROSTRUCTURE
Course Introduction
===============================================================================

Definition
----------

Market Microstructure is the study of how financial markets actually
operate and how prices are formed through the interaction of market
participants.

Unlike traditional financial theory, Market Microstructure focuses on
the real-world mechanics of trading.

===============================================================================
COURSE OBJECTIVE
===============================================================================

Develop an understanding of:

    • How financial markets function.

    • How prices are formed.

    • How liquidity affects markets.

    • How market design influences trading.

    • How information becomes reflected in prices.

    • How technology and regulation change market behavior.

===============================================================================
LEARNING OUTCOMES
===============================================================================

By the end of this course, you should understand:

Liquidity

    • What liquidity is.

    • How liquidity is measured.

    • What determines market liquidity.

    • Why liquidity changes.

Price Formation

    • How buyers and sellers determine prices.

    • How trades affect price movement.

    • How information becomes incorporated into prices.

Market Structure

    • How exchanges operate.

    • How trading platforms are designed.

    • How market design affects liquidity.

    • How market design affects trading efficiency.

Technology

    • Algorithmic Trading.

    • High-Frequency Trading (HFT).

    • Computer-driven markets.

    • Modern electronic exchanges.

===============================================================================
ASSET PRICING VS MARKET MICROSTRUCTURE
===============================================================================

Asset Pricing

    Assumption

        • Markets are frictionless.

    Focus

        • The theoretical value of an asset.

    Main Question

        • "What should this asset be worth?"

------------------------------------------------------------

Market Microstructure

    Assumption

        • Markets contain imperfections.

    Focus

        • The actual trading process.

    Main Question

        • "How does the market arrive at this price?"

===============================================================================
MARKET IMPERFECTIONS (FRICTIONS)
===============================================================================

Real financial markets contain frictions such as:

    • Bid-Ask Spreads

    • Transaction Costs

    • Limited Liquidity

    • Information Asymmetry

    • Delays in Price Discovery

These frictions influence:

    • Asset Prices

    • Execution Quality

    • Trading Costs

    • Market Efficiency

===============================================================================
WHY MARKET MICROSTRUCTURE MATTERS
===============================================================================

Understanding market microstructure helps explain:

    • Why prices move.

    • Why liquidity disappears.

    • Why spreads widen.

    • Why execution quality changes.

    • Why some markets are more efficient than others.

    • How new information affects prices.

===============================================================================
APPLICATIONS
===============================================================================

Market Microstructure is fundamental for:

    • Quantitative Trading

    • Market Analytics

    • Order Book Analysis

    • Liquidity Research

    • Algorithmic Trading

    • High-Frequency Trading

    • Financial Market Research

===============================================================================
KEY TAKEAWAY
===============================================================================

Market Microstructure studies the real-world mechanics of financial
markets.

Rather than focusing only on an asset's theoretical value, it seeks to
understand:

    • How prices are formed.

    • How markets function.

    • How liquidity affects trading.

    • How market participants interact.

    • How technology and market design influence price discovery.

Understanding these mechanisms provides the foundation for developing
better market research, quantitative models, and trading systems.
