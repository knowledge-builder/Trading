===============================================================================
FINANCIAL ASSETS
===============================================================================

Definition
----------

A financial asset is something that has economic value and can be bought,
sold, or traded in a financial market.

Different financial assets represent different legal relationships between
the buyer and the issuer.

===============================================================================
1. STOCKS
===============================================================================

Definition

    A stock represents partial ownership of a company.

When you buy a stock, you become a shareholder and own a small portion
of the business.

Examples

    • Apple (AAPL)

    • Microsoft (MSFT)

    • NVIDIA (NVDA)

Purpose

    • Ownership

    • Capital Appreciation

    • Dividend Income

Investor Relationship

    Investor

        ↓

    Partial Owner of Company

Returns

    • Stock Price Appreciation

    • Dividends

===============================================================================
2. BONDS
===============================================================================

Definition

    A bond represents a loan made to a government or corporation.

Instead of becoming an owner, the investor becomes a lender.

Examples

    • U.S. Treasury Bonds

    • Corporate Bonds

Purpose

    • Interest Income

    • Capital Preservation

    • Lower Investment Risk

Investor Relationship

    Investor

        ↓

    Lender

        ↓

    Government / Corporation

Returns

    • Interest Payments

    • Return of Principal at Maturity

===============================================================================
3. FUTURES
===============================================================================

Definition

    A futures contract is a standardized agreement to buy or sell an
    asset at a predetermined price on a future date.

A futures contract is NOT ownership of the underlying asset.

It is a legally binding contract.

Underlying Assets

    • Gold

    • Crude Oil

    • Corn

    • Stock Indices

    • Currencies

Purpose

    • Hedging

    • Speculation

    • Price Discovery

Relationship

    Buyer

        ↔

    Futures Contract

        ↔

    Seller

Returns

    • Profit or Loss from Price Changes

===============================================================================
4. OPTIONS
===============================================================================

Definition

    An option gives the holder the RIGHT, but NOT the obligation,
    to buy or sell an asset at a specified price before or on a
    specified expiration date.

Types

    • Call Option

        Right to Buy

    • Put Option

        Right to Sell

Purpose

    • Hedging

    • Speculation

    • Income Generation

Relationship

    Option Buyer

        ↓

    Purchases a Right

        ↓

    Chooses Whether to Exercise It

Returns

    • Profit if exercised advantageously

    • Maximum Loss generally limited to the premium paid by the buyer

===============================================================================
5. CURRENCIES
===============================================================================

Definition

    A currency is the official money issued by a country or monetary
    union.

Currencies are traded in exchange pairs.

Examples

    • USD - United States Dollar

    • EUR - Euro

    • JPY - Japanese Yen

    • GBP - British Pound

Purpose

    • International Trade

    • Foreign Exchange

    • Investment

    • Travel

Relationship

    Currency A

        ↔

    Currency B

Returns

    • Profit or Loss from Exchange Rate Changes


===============================================================================
6. CRYPTOCURRENCIES
===============================================================================

Definition

    A cryptocurrency is a digital asset that uses cryptography and
    blockchain technology to enable peer-to-peer transactions without
    requiring a central authority.

Unlike traditional currencies, most cryptocurrencies are decentralized
and operate on distributed ledger technology.

Examples

    • Bitcoin (BTC)

    • Ethereum (ETH)

    • Solana (SOL)

    • XRP

Purpose

    • Digital Payments

    • Store of Value

    • Smart Contracts

    • Decentralized Finance (DeFi)

    • Speculation

Investor Relationship

    Investor

        ↓

    Owns Digital Tokens

Returns

    • Price Appreciation

    • Staking Rewards (for some cryptocurrencies)

    • Participation in Blockchain Networks
	

===============================================================================
SUMMARY
===============================================================================

Stocks

    Represents

        • Ownership

Relationship

        • Investor becomes a shareholder.

Primary Goal

        • Growth and dividends.

------------------------------------------------------------

Bonds

    Represents

        • A Loan

Relationship

        • Investor becomes a lender.

Primary Goal

        • Interest income and capital preservation.

------------------------------------------------------------

Futures

    Represents

        • A Contract

Relationship

        • Agreement to buy or sell later.

Primary Goal

        • Hedging and speculation.

------------------------------------------------------------

Options

    Represents

        • A Right

Relationship

        • Right to buy or sell without obligation.

Primary Goal

        • Hedging and speculation.

------------------------------------------------------------

Currencies

    Represents

        • Money

Relationship

        • Exchange between different national currencies.

Primary Goal

        • Trade, investment, and foreign exchange.

===============================================================================
KEY TAKEAWAY
===============================================================================

Every financial asset represents a different legal relationship.

    • Stock          → Ownership

    • Bond           → Loan

    • Future         → Contract

    • Option         → Right

    • Currency       → Money
	
	• Cryptocurrency → Digital Asset

Understanding what each asset represents is the first step toward
understanding how financial markets operate and how prices are formed.
