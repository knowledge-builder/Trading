===============================================================================
ASSET PRICING VS MARKET MICROSTRUCTURE
===============================================================================

Although both fields study financial markets, they answer different
questions.

Asset Pricing focuses on an asset's theoretical value.

Market Microstructure focuses on how that value becomes the observed
market price.

===============================================================================
CLASSICAL FINANCE (ASSET PRICING)
===============================================================================

Primary Question

    "What should this asset be worth?"

Core Assumption

    Markets are perfect.

Assumptions include:

    • Everyone has access to the same information.

    • Everyone can trade freely.

    • There are no trading costs.

    • There are no bid-ask spreads.

    • No participant is large enough to influence prices.

    • Prices reflect the consensus view of investors.

Price Formation

    Asset Fundamentals

            ↓

       Fair Value

            ↓

       Market Price

Focus

    • Future Cash Flows

    • Risk

    • Discount Rates

    • Intrinsic Value

Objective

    Estimate the fair or theoretical value of an asset.

===============================================================================
MARKET MICROSTRUCTURE
===============================================================================

Primary Question

    "How does the market actually arrive at this price?"

Core Assumption

    Markets are NOT perfect.

Real-world markets contain imperfections that influence prices.

Examples

    • Bid-Ask Spreads

    • Trading Costs

    • Liquidity Constraints

    • Information Asymmetry

    • Order Flow

    • Market Participants

    • Exchange Rules

    • Trading Technology

Price Formation

    Fundamental Value

            +

    Order Flow

            +

    Liquidity

            +

    Market Frictions

            +

    Trading Activity

            ↓

    Observed Market Price

Focus

    • Price Discovery

    • Order Book

    • Liquidity

    • Market Makers

    • Trading Costs

    • Execution Quality

Objective

    Understand how trading mechanics determine the prices observed in
    financial markets.

===============================================================================
COMPARISON
===============================================================================

Asset Pricing

    Studies

        • What an asset should be worth.

    Assumes

        • Perfect markets.

    Main Concern

        • Fair value.

------------------------------------------------------------

Market Microstructure

    Studies

        • How prices are actually formed.

    Assumes

        • Imperfect markets.

    Main Concern

        • Trading process.

===============================================================================
KEY INSIGHT
===============================================================================

Asset Pricing explains:

    "Why an asset has value."

Market Microstructure explains:

    "How buyers and sellers, through trading, produce the price observed
     in the market."

Both fields complement each other.

Asset Pricing provides the theoretical value.

Market Microstructure explains the real-world process by which that
value is reflected in market prices.
