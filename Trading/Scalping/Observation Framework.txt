==============================================================================
Market Microstructure Observation Framework
==============================================================================

Project Milestone:
Building a real-time market behavior observation system using exchange data.

Purpose:
--------
This project focuses on understanding market behavior by observing the
interaction between:

    1. Price movement
    2. Aggressive order flow
    3. Order book liquidity pressure
    4. Candle response

The objective is not to predict the market directly, but to study how buying
and selling pressure develops and how price reacts.

==============================================================================
Core Idea
==============================================================================

A candle is only the final output of market activity.

Behind every candle exists a continuous battle between:

    - Traders aggressively buying into available liquidity
    - Traders aggressively selling into available liquidity
    - Passive liquidity waiting in the order book
    - Market participants absorbing incoming pressure

This framework attempts to observe that interaction.

==============================================================================
Data Components
==============================================================================

1. OHLCV Data
--------------

Purpose:
    Provides the visible result of market activity.

Captured:

    Open
    High
    Low
    Close
    Volume
    Amount


Interpretation:

    The candle shows what happened.

    It does not explain why it happened.


Example:

    Strong bullish candle

    Possible causes:

        - Strong aggressive buying
        - Weak selling pressure
        - Liquidity absorption
        - Short covering


==============================================================================
2. Aggressive Buy / Sell Flow
==============================================================================

Purpose:
    Measure market participants who actively consume liquidity.

Aggressive Buy:

    Market buyers crossing the spread and lifting asks.


Aggressive Sell:

    Market sellers crossing the spread and hitting bids.


Metrics:

    batch_buy_value
    batch_sell_value
    trade_delta
    cumulative_delta


Interpretation:


    Positive Delta:

        Buyers are consuming more liquidity.


    Negative Delta:

        Sellers are consuming more liquidity.



Example:

    Aggressive Buy:

        $800,000


    Aggressive Sell:

        $200,000


    Delta:

        +$600,000


Observation:

    Strong buying pressure is present.

    Next question:

        Can this pressure move price higher?


==============================================================================
3. Order Book Wall Pressure
==============================================================================

Purpose:
    Observe available liquidity that can support or resist movement.


Measured By:

    Largest Bid Wall
    Largest Ask Wall


Example:


    Bid Wall:

        $2,000,000


    Ask Wall:

        $500,000


    Pressure Ratio:

        4x Buy Pressure



Interpretation:

    Higher Bid Wall:

        More visible support liquidity.


    Higher Ask Wall:

        More visible resistance liquidity.



Important:

    Liquidity does not guarantee movement.

    A large wall may:

        - Absorb pressure
        - Disappear
        - Be tested repeatedly


==============================================================================
Decision Framework
==============================================================================

The analysis combines three layers:


Layer 1:
--------
Aggressive Flow

Question:

    Who is actively attacking?


Example:

    Buyers are aggressively buying.



Layer 2:
--------
Liquidity Pressure

Question:

    Is there enough liquidity support or resistance?


Example:

    Buyers have stronger bid walls.



Layer 3:
--------
Price Response

Question:

    Did the market actually react?


Example:

    5-minute candle closes near the high.



==============================================================================
Example Observation
==============================================================================


Scenario:

    Aggressive Buy Flow:

        Strong increase


    Wall Pressure:

        Buy side stronger


    Candle:

        5-minute candle closes bullish



Interpretation:

    Buyers are active.

    Liquidity supports the move.

    Price confirms the pressure.



==============================================================================
Important Market Behavior Patterns
==============================================================================


Pattern 1:
----------

Aggressive Buy
+
Strong Bid Pressure
+
Bullish Candle Response


Possible Interpretation:

    Buyers are controlling the auction.



Pattern 2:
----------

Aggressive Buy
+
Strong Ask Pressure
+
Weak Candle Response


Possible Interpretation:

    Sellers may be absorbing buying pressure.



Pattern 3:
----------

Aggressive Sell
+
Strong Bid Support
+
Price Refuses To Drop


Possible Interpretation:

    Possible accumulation or absorption.



==============================================================================
Current Trading Philosophy
==============================================================================

The goal is not to predict every candle.

The goal is to understand:

    "What pressure is currently entering the market,
     where liquidity exists,
     and how price responds."



The market should be observed before it is acted upon.
