==============================================================================
LIQUIDITY SPACE
==============================================================================

Definition

    Liquidity Space represents the available buying and selling interest
    currently resting in the order book.

    Unlike executed trades, liquidity represents potential future trades.

Research Objective

    Measure how liquidity changes over time and investigate its influence
    on price movement.

Fundamental Principle

    Price does not move because of Trade Delta alone.

    Price emerges from the interaction between

        • Available Liquidity
        • Aggressive Orders
        • Order Cancellations
        • Newly Added Liquidity

==============================================================================
1. BEST BID LIQUIDITY
==============================================================================

Definition

    Quantity available at the highest bid price.

Interpretation

    • Immediate buying support.
    • First liquidity consumed by Market Sell orders.

Example

    Price        Quantity

    1899.99      120 ETH

Variable

    Best Bid Liquidity = 120 ETH

==============================================================================
2. BEST ASK LIQUIDITY
==============================================================================

Definition

    Quantity available at the lowest ask price.

Interpretation

    • Immediate selling pressure.
    • First liquidity consumed by Market Buy orders.

Example

    Price        Quantity

    1900.00      85 ETH

Variable

    Best Ask Liquidity = 85 ETH

==============================================================================
3. TOTAL BID LIQUIDITY
==============================================================================

Definition

    Sum of all bid quantities within the selected depth.

Formula

    Total Bid Liquidity

        =

    Σ(Bid Quantities)

Interpretation

    • Overall buying support.
    • Larger values indicate stronger demand.

==============================================================================
4. TOTAL ASK LIQUIDITY
==============================================================================

Definition

    Sum of all ask quantities within the selected depth.

Formula

    Total Ask Liquidity

        =

    Σ(Ask Quantities)

Interpretation

    • Overall selling pressure.
    • Larger values indicate greater available supply.

==============================================================================
5. LIQUIDITY IMBALANCE
==============================================================================

Definition

    Measures the proportion of buy-side liquidity relative to the total
    available liquidity.

Formula

    Liquidity Imbalance

        =

    Bid Liquidity

        /

    (Bid Liquidity + Ask Liquidity)

Range

    0 → Entirely Ask Liquidity

    0.5 → Balanced

    1 → Entirely Bid Liquidity

Interpretation

    > 0.50

        Buy-side liquidity dominates.

    < 0.50

        Sell-side liquidity dominates.

==============================================================================
6. LIQUIDITY WALL
==============================================================================

Definition

    A price level containing an unusually large resting quantity.

Interpretation

    • Potential support.
    • Potential resistance.
    • May attract or repel price.
    • May disappear through cancellation.

Example

    Price        Quantity

    1900.02      1200 ETH

==============================================================================
7. DISTANCE TO LIQUIDITY WALL
==============================================================================

Definition

    Distance between the current price and the nearest significant
    liquidity wall.

Formula

    Distance

        =

    |Current Price − Wall Price|

Interpretation

    • Smaller distance means price is approaching major liquidity.
    • Larger distance indicates open price space.

==============================================================================
8. LIQUIDITY ADDED
==============================================================================

Definition

    New resting limit orders added to the order book.

Formula

    Liquidity Added

        =

    Current Quantity

        −

    Previous Quantity

Interpretation

    • Positive values indicate new liquidity entering the market.

==============================================================================
9. LIQUIDITY REMOVED
==============================================================================

Definition

    Liquidity removed from the order book.

Possible Causes

    • Trade Execution
    • Order Cancellation

Interpretation

    Liquidity removal alone does not indicate which event occurred.

==============================================================================
10. CANCELLATION RATE
==============================================================================

Definition

    Rate at which resting orders disappear without execution.

Formula

    Cancellation Rate

        =

    Cancelled Quantity

        /

    Time

Research Question

    Does an increase in cancellation activity precede higher volatility?

==============================================================================
11. LIQUIDITY CONSUMPTION RATE
==============================================================================

Definition

    Rate at which aggressive orders consume available liquidity.

Formula

    Consumption Rate

        =

    Executed Quantity

        /

    Time

Interpretation

    • Higher values indicate faster liquidity depletion.

==============================================================================
12. LIQUIDITY VELOCITY
==============================================================================

Definition

    Net rate of liquidity change over time.

Formula

    Liquidity Velocity

        =

    Liquidity Added

        −

    Liquidity Removed

Interpretation

    Positive

        Liquidity is accumulating.

    Negative

        Liquidity is disappearing.

MENTAL MODEL
==============================================================================

Trader Decisions

    • Place Limit Order
    • Cancel Order
    • Submit Market Order

            │

            ▼

Order Book Changes

            │

            ▼

Liquidity Changes

            │

            ▼

Trade Executions

            │

            ▼

Price Movement

Research Principle

    Do not ask

        "What determines price?"

    Ask

        "How do changes in liquidity influence price movement?"

    Price is an emergent property of market interactions rather than a
    directly calculated variable.
