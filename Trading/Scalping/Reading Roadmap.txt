Recommended Reading Roadmap
===========================

This roadmap is organized from foundational market knowledge to
advanced quantitative detection of market manipulation.

====================================================================
Tier 1 — Foundation (Must Read)
====================================================================

1. Trades, Quotes and Prices
   Author: Larry Harris
   Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Purpose
    The best starting point for understanding why market
    manipulation works.

Topics
    • Order books
    • Liquidity
    • Market participants
    • Information asymmetry
    • Market impact
    • Price discovery

Why Read It
    Without understanding market microstructure, it is difficult to
    distinguish manipulation from normal market behavior.


2. Trading and Exchanges
   Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Purpose
    A comprehensive textbook covering how modern electronic markets
    operate.

Topics
    • Exchange mechanics
    • Order types
    • Liquidity providers
    • Market abuse concepts
    • Electronic trading systems

Why Read It
    Provides the operational knowledge needed to understand how
    exchanges function and how manipulation can occur.

====================================================================
Tier 2 — Manipulation & Market Behavior
====================================================================

1. Flash Boys
   Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐☆

Focus
    • High-frequency trading (HFT)
    • Exchange incentives
    • Latency advantages
    • Market structure

Why Read It
    Demonstrates how incentives and technology influence market
    behavior, even though the examples focus on traditional markets.


2. Dark Pools
   Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐☆

Focus
    • Hidden liquidity
    • Institutional execution
    • Information leakage
    • Market transparency

Why Read It
    Explains how large institutional participants interact with
    markets and how hidden liquidity affects price formation.

====================================================================
Tier 3 — Crypto-Specific
====================================================================

1. Mastering Bitcoin

Purpose
    Build a strong understanding of blockchain fundamentals.

Topics
    • Blockchain
    • Mempool
    • Transaction propagation
    • Mining
    • Network mechanics

Why Read It
    Essential for understanding on-chain market behavior.


2. Mastering Ethereum

Purpose
    Understand decentralized finance (DeFi) and Ethereum's execution
    model.

Topics
    • Smart contracts
    • DeFi
    • Oracles
    • Maximal Extractable Value (MEV)
    • Transaction ordering

Why Read It
    Important for studying manipulation within decentralized
    financial systems.

====================================================================
Tier 4 — Quantitative Detection
====================================================================

1. Algorithmic Trading and DMA

Focus
    • Order routing
    • Execution algorithms
    • Market impact
    • Electronic trading infrastructure

Why Read It
    Provides the foundation for building tools that analyze order
    flow and execution quality.


2. Market Microstructure Theory

Focus
    • Information asymmetry
    • Dealer behavior
    • Liquidity models
    • Informed trading

Why Read It
    A rigorous academic treatment of market microstructure concepts.
    Recommended after reading Larry Harris.

====================================================================
Beyond Books
====================================================================

Books provide theoretical foundations, but practical knowledge about
market manipulation often comes from real-world research and case
studies.

Recommended Sources

• Academic papers on:
    • Market surveillance
    • Order-book manipulation
    • Market microstructure
    • Liquidity analysis

• Regulatory enforcement actions
    • Spoofing investigations
    • Wash trading cases
    • Pump-and-dump schemes
    • Insider trading investigations

• Cryptocurrency exchange research
    • Wash trading detection
    • Spoofing detection
    • Market surveillance
    • Trading analytics

• Exchange surveillance documentation
    • Technical reports
    • Detection methodologies
    • Surveillance frameworks

These sources frequently include

    • Order timelines
    • Execution patterns
    • Surveillance methodologies
    • Evidence used during investigations
    • Real-world manipulation examples

====================================================================
Recommended Reading Order
====================================================================

1. Trades, Quotes and Prices
   Foundation in market microstructure.

2. Trading and Exchanges
   Exchange mechanics and electronic markets.

3. Flash Boys
   Electronic market behavior and incentives.

4. Dark Pools
   Institutional trading and hidden liquidity.

5. Mastering Bitcoin
   Blockchain infrastructure.

6. Mastering Ethereum
   DeFi, smart contracts, and MEV.

7. Algorithmic Trading and DMA
   Execution algorithms and order-flow analysis.

8. Market Microstructure Theory
   Advanced academic understanding.

====================================================================
Overall Recommendation
====================================================================

For anyone studying spoofing, layering, wash trading, pump-and-dump
schemes, liquidity manipulation, or broader crypto market abuse,
combine these books with:

• Academic research papers
• Regulatory enforcement case studies
• Exchange surveillance reports
• Technical documentation

These resources often provide more practical insight into manipulation
than books alone because they analyze real-world incidents and the
evidence used to identify abusive market behavior.
