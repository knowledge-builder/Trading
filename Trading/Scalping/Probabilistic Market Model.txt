===============================================================================
PROBABILISTIC MARKET STATE MODEL
A Probability-Driven Decision Framework
===============================================================================

Core Philosophy
---------------

The objective of the system is NOT to predict price with certainty.

Instead, the objective is to continuously estimate the probability of a future
market outcome based on the interaction of multiple market observations.

Unlike traditional rule-based systems:

    IF Delta > 0 THEN BUY

our approach treats every observation as evidence that changes our belief about
the current market state.

The probability is therefore continuously updated as new information arrives.

Example

    P(Price moves UP within the next 1 minute)

    50%
        ↓
    Positive Trade Delta
        ↓
    57%
        ↓
    Spread Narrows
        ↓
    63%
        ↓
    Large Sell Wall Appears
        ↓
    49%
        ↓
    Sell Wall Weakens
        ↓
    67%

The probability evolves together with the market.

===============================================================================
Prediction vs Outcome
===============================================================================

Prediction

    Time
    ----

    10:01:15

    P(Price Up in 1 minute)

    = 62%

Outcome

    Time
    ----

    10:02:15

    Price actually moved DOWN

Prediction

    Incorrect

The purpose of recording every prediction is not to determine whether a single
trade was successful, but to evaluate the quality of the probability model over
many observations.

===============================================================================
Probability Calibration
===============================================================================

The model should not only generate probabilities.

It must also measure whether those probabilities accurately represent reality.

Suppose the model produced

    P(UP) = 62%

five hundred different times.

Observed Result

    Price increased

    310 times

Calibration

    310 / 500

    = 62%

This indicates that the model is well-calibrated.

If instead

    180 / 500

    = 36%

then the model is overconfident.

If

    450 / 500

    = 90%

then the model is underconfident.

A calibrated model is more valuable than a model that simply reports arbitrary
confidence values.

===============================================================================
Why Calibration Matters
===============================================================================

Traditional Trading Question

    "Did this trade win?"

Probability-Based Question

    "When my model predicts a 62% probability,
     does the market actually move upward
     approximately 62% of the time?"

These are fundamentally different questions.

The first evaluates an individual trade.

The second evaluates the quality of the predictive model.

===============================================================================
Important Principle
===============================================================================

The live trading engine and the learning engine should remain separate.

Live Engine

    Purpose

        Produce real-time probability estimates.

Offline Learning Engine

    Purpose

        Evaluate historical predictions.

        Measure calibration.

        Improve model parameters.

This separation ensures that the live model remains stable while allowing
continuous improvement based on accumulated market evidence.

===============================================================================
Research Objective
===============================================================================

The objective is NOT to maximize the number of winning trades.

The objective is to construct a probabilistic market model whose estimated
probabilities closely match the true frequencies observed in the market.

Success is measured by calibration.

When the model predicts

    70%

the market should move upward approximately 70% of the time over a sufficiently
large number of observations.

Only after achieving a well-calibrated probability model should trading
decisions be optimized.

In this framework,

    Prediction
        ↓
    Calibration
        ↓
    Decision

rather than

    Prediction
        ↓
    Immediate Trade

This shifts the focus from reacting to individual trades toward building a
reliable probabilistic understanding of market behavior.
