===============================================================================
SCALPING RESEARCH FRAMEWORK
A Market Microstructure Approach
===============================================================================

Objective
---------

Develop expertise in short-term market behavior by understanding the
underlying mechanics that produce price movement.

The objective is NOT to predict price using indicators alone.

Instead, the objective is to understand

    • Buying Decisions

    • Selling Decisions

    • Liquidity

    • Order Flow

    • Market Constraints

that collectively produce short-term price movement.

Time Horizon
------------

    • Seconds

    • Tens of Seconds

    • A Few Minutes

This framework is designed exclusively for scalping.

===============================================================================
Core Philosophy
===============================================================================

A long-term investor asks

    "Where will price be next month?"

A swing trader asks

    "Will this trend continue?"

A scalper asks

    "What is the highest probability movement over
     the next few seconds or minutes?"

The unit of analysis is no longer the daily trend.

It becomes the continuous interaction between buyers and sellers.

===============================================================================
The Order Book
===============================================================================

The order book is NOT a trading signal.

It is a snapshot of the current battlefield.

It represents

    • Current Buy Interest

    • Current Sell Interest

    • Available Liquidity

    • Immediate Market Constraints

It does NOT directly tell us

    • Buy

    • Sell

Instead, it provides information about the current market structure.

===============================================================================
Research Mindset
===============================================================================

Do not immediately ask

    "Should I Buy?"

or

    "Should I Sell?"

Instead ask

    "What is currently happening?"

The purpose is observation before decision.

	===============================================================================
	Observation Framework
	===============================================================================

	Liquidity

		• Is buy liquidity increasing?

		• Is sell liquidity increasing?

		• Is liquidity disappearing?

		• Is liquidity being replenished?

	Order Flow

		• Are aggressive buyers arriving?

		• Are aggressive sellers arriving?

		• Which side is consuming liquidity faster?

	Market Constraints

		• Can buyers overcome the current sell liquidity?

		• Can sellers overcome the current buy liquidity?

		• Where are the largest liquidity concentrations?

	Market Evolution

		• Does the liquidity remain?

		• Does it move?

		• Does it disappear?

		• Does it get cancelled?

		• Does it get executed?

	Spread

		• Is the spread widening?

		• Is the spread narrowing?

===============================================================================
Research Workflow
===============================================================================

Phase 1
Observation

Collect

    • Order Book Snapshots

    • Order Book Updates

    • Trade Executions

    • Exchange Timestamp

    • Local Receive Timestamp

Purpose

    Build an accurate record of market activity.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Phase 2
Event Detection

Detect events such as

    • Large Buy Wall Appears

    • Large Sell Wall Appears

    • Buy Wall Cancelled

    • Sell Wall Cancelled

    • Buy Wall Executed

    • Sell Wall Executed

    • Aggressive Buying

    • Aggressive Selling

    • Increasing Buy Liquidity

    • Increasing Sell Liquidity

Purpose

    Transform raw market data into observable market events.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Phase 3
Outcome Measurement

Measure

    • Price after 1 second

    • Price after 5 seconds

    • Price after 10 seconds

    • Price after 30 seconds

    • Maximum Favorable Movement

    • Maximum Adverse Movement

Purpose

    Determine how the market responded after each event.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Phase 4
Hypothesis Testing

Examples

    • Persistent buy walls increase the probability
      of short-term upward movement.

    • Persistent sell walls increase the probability
      of short-term downward movement.

    • Large buy wall cancellations precede
      downward price movement.

    • Large sell wall cancellations precede
      upward price movement.

    • Repeated aggressive buying eventually
      consumes resistance.

    • Repeated aggressive selling eventually
      consumes support.

Purpose

    Replace intuition with measurable evidence.

===============================================================================
Static vs Dynamic Thinking
===============================================================================

Weak Question

    "There is a large buy wall.

     Will price go up?"

Better Question

    "How does the buy wall evolve over time?"

Even Better Question

    "How does the interaction between liquidity
     and aggressive market orders influence
     short-term price movement?"

The objective is not to analyze static snapshots.

The objective is to analyze continuous market evolution.

===============================================================================
Constraint-Oriented Thinking
===============================================================================

Instead of asking

    "Will price move upward?"

Ask

    "What must happen before price can move upward?"

Examples

    • Buy liquidity must overcome sell liquidity.

    • Sell wall must be consumed.

    • Sell wall must be cancelled.

    • Additional buyers must enter.

Likewise

Instead of asking

    "Will price move downward?"

Ask

    "What must happen before price can move downward?"

Examples

    • Buy liquidity must be consumed.

    • Buy wall must disappear.

    • Aggressive sellers must dominate order flow.

Thinking in terms of constraints encourages
mechanistic rather than speculative reasoning.

===============================================================================
Scientific Principle
===============================================================================

     Observe

        ↓

     Measure

        ↓

   Detect Events

        ↓

  Form Hypothesis

        ↓

  Test Hypothesis

        ↓

  Evaluate Evidence

        ↓

Improve Understanding

===============================================================================
Ultimate Objective
===============================================================================

Develop intuition that is grounded in measurable market behavior.

The goal is not merely to predict direction.

The goal is to understand the underlying mechanics that produce

    • Buying Pressure

    • Selling Pressure

    • Liquidity Changes

    • Order Flow

    • Market Constraints

    • Short-Term Price Movement

A skilled scalper should not react to price alone.

A skilled scalper should understand the market processes
that continuously generate price.
