Market Manipulation Documentation
=================================

A Better Way to Study Manipulation
----------------------------------
Instead of organizing manipulation by strategy, organize it by the
market illusion being created. This makes detection more practical
because algorithms can focus on observable market behavior.

1. Fake Liquidity
-----------------

Objective
    Convince other participants that significant buying or selling
    interest exists.

Examples
    • Spoofing
    • Layering
    • Fleeting orders

Observe
    • Order additions
    • Order cancellations
    • Order lifetime
    • Cancellation ratio
    • Order wall persistence

Python Analysis Ideas
    • Measure order lifetime.
    • Calculate cancellation ratios.
    • Detect large orders removed before execution.
    • Track persistent vs. short-lived liquidity walls.


2. Fake Demand
--------------

Objective
    Make the market appear more active than it actually is.

Examples
    • Wash trading
    • Self-trading
    • Coordinated trading

Observe
    • Repeated counterparties (when available)
    • High volume with little price movement
    • Temporary volume spikes

Python Analysis Ideas
    • Compare volume against price movement.
    • Detect recurring trade patterns.
    • Identify abnormal volume spikes.
    • Compute rolling volume statistics.


3. Liquidity Traps
------------------

Objective
    Encourage traders to enter positions that become profitable targets.

Examples
    • Stop-loss hunting
    • Liquidity sweeps
    • False breakouts

Observe
    • Liquidity cluster locations
    • Price acceptance or rejection
    • Volume around the event
    • Execution behavior

Python Analysis Ideas
    • Identify liquidity zones.
    • Detect breakout failures.
    • Measure reversal probability.
    • Analyze execution around key price levels.


4. Information Manipulation
---------------------------

Objective
    Influence market expectations instead of the order book.

Examples
    • Pump-and-dump campaigns
    • False announcements
    • Coordinated social media activity

Observe
    • News timing relative to price
    • Volume before announcements
    • Cross-platform sentiment

Python Analysis Ideas
    • Align news with market timestamps.
    • Detect unusual pre-announcement volume.
    • Perform sentiment analysis.
    • Compare sentiment changes with price reactions.


Summary
-------

• Fake Liquidity
    • Goal: Create the illusion of available liquidity.
    • Metrics:
        • Order lifetime
        • Cancellation ratio
        • Wall persistence

• Fake Demand
    • Goal: Create the illusion of strong market activity.
    • Metrics:
        • Volume anomalies
        • Price-volume divergence
        • Repeated trading patterns

• Liquidity Traps
    • Goal: Lure traders into vulnerable positions.
    • Metrics:
        • Liquidity zones
        • Breakout success/failure
        • Reversal behavior

• Information Manipulation
    • Goal: Influence expectations through external information.
    • Metrics:
        • News timing
        • Sentiment
        • Pre-event trading volume


Design Principles
-----------------

• Focus on observable market behavior.
• Detect anomalies rather than specific strategies.
• Combine order book, trade, volume, price, and external data.
• Treat each category as a measurable market illusion.
