================================================================================
FOUNDATIONAL INSIGHT
One Market, Multiple Perspectives
================================================================================

Objective
---------
Understand the relationship between:

    • Spot Traders
    • Derivatives Traders
    • Market Price
    • Trends

A common misconception is that Spot and Derivatives operate in completely
different worlds.

They do not.

They participate in the same financial ecosystem from different perspectives.

================================================================================
THE MARKET IS ONE ECOSYSTEM
================================================================================

Think of the financial market as one planet.

                    FINANCIAL MARKET

        • Spot Traders

        • Derivatives Traders

        • Market Makers

        • Institutions

        • High Frequency Traders (HFT)

        • Arbitrageurs

        • Retail Traders

All participants coexist within the same ecosystem.

The difference lies in:

    • Their objectives.

    • Their financial instruments.

    • Their decision-making process.

NOT

    • The market itself.

================================================================================
DIFFERENT PERSPECTIVES
================================================================================

Spot Trader

Primary Question

    "Do I want to own this asset?"

Objective

    • Acquire ownership.

    • Sell later at a higher price.

------------------------------------------------------------

Derivatives Trader

Primary Question

    "Which direction do I believe price will move?"

Objective

    • Open a Position.

    • Profit from future price movement.

Ownership of the underlying asset is not required.

================================================================================
THE IMPORTANT MENTAL SHIFT
================================================================================

As a derivatives trader,

my primary objective is NOT

    "Should I own Bitcoin?"

Instead it becomes

    "Should I open a Long or Short position?"

The focus shifts from

    Asset Ownership

to

    Market Exposure.

================================================================================
WHAT IS A TREND?
================================================================================

Question

    When observing a bullish or bearish trend,

    am I only observing traditional buying and selling
    of the underlying asset?

Answer

    No.

A market trend is NOT produced exclusively by:

    • Spot Traders

Nor exclusively by:

    • Derivatives Traders

Instead,

a trend emerges from the interaction of ALL market participants.

================================================================================
EMERGENT BEHAVIOR
================================================================================

Think of the market as a living system.

                Spot Traders
                       │

                Derivatives Traders
                       │

                Institutions
                       │

                Market Makers
                       │

                HFT Algorithms
                       │

                Arbitrageurs
                       │

                       ▼

                 Order Flow
                       │

                       ▼

           Liquidity Interactions
                       │

                       ▼

               Price Movement
                       │

                       ▼

                 Market Trend

A trend is therefore

    An emergent behavior

resulting from continuous interactions
between all market participants.

================================================================================
THE ROLE OF A DERIVATIVES TRADER
================================================================================

As a derivatives trader,

I am NOT necessarily attempting
to create the trend.

Instead,

I ask

    • Is there an opportunity?

    • Which direction provides the better probability?

    • Should I open:

        • Long

        or

        • Short

The objective is to determine
which position provides the most favorable
risk-reward opportunity.

================================================================================
A POSITION IS A HYPOTHESIS
================================================================================

Rather than thinking

    "I'm buying Bitcoin."

Think

    "I'm expressing a hypothesis
     about future price movement."

------------------------------------------------------------

Long Position

    Hypothesis

        "I believe price will increase."

------------------------------------------------------------

Short Position

    Hypothesis

        "I believe price will decrease."

Every position represents a market hypothesis.

================================================================================
DO DERIVATIVES TRADERS AFFECT PRICE?
================================================================================

Yes.

Every submitted order participates in the market.

However,

the magnitude of influence depends on:

    • Position Size

    • Available Liquidity

    • Current Market Conditions

Examples

Small Retail Position

    • Minimal market impact.

Large Institutional Position

    • Potentially significant impact.

Therefore,

derivatives traders are active market participants,

not passive observers.

================================================================================
FIRST-PRINCIPLES INSIGHT
================================================================================

There are NOT two separate markets.

There is ONE market ecosystem viewed
from different perspectives.

------------------------------------------------------------

Spot Perspective

    Focus

        Asset Ownership

------------------------------------------------------------

Derivatives Perspective

    Focus

        Market Exposure

------------------------------------------------------------

Both ultimately interact within the same
price discovery process.

================================================================================
KEY TAKEAWAYS
================================================================================

• Spot and Derivatives exist within the same market ecosystem.

• Different participants pursue different objectives,
  but all contribute to overall market behavior.

• Trends are emergent phenomena resulting from
  continuous interactions among all market participants.

• A derivatives trader is not primarily concerned
  with owning an asset.

• A derivatives trader is concerned with selecting
  the appropriate market position (Long or Short).

• Every position represents a hypothesis about
  future price movement.

• Every submitted order contributes to market
  interactions and, collectively, to price discovery.
