================================================================================
FIRST-PRINCIPLES INSIGHT
Market Movement Is The Result Of Interacting Decision Makers
================================================================================

The market does not move by itself.

Markets move because participants continuously make decisions.

Every participant has

    • Objectives
    • Constraints
    • Information
    • Decision Rules

The interaction of those decisions produces

    • Order Flow
    • Liquidity Changes
    • Price Movement
    • Trends
    • Reversals
    • Volatility

================================================================================
EVERY PARTICIPANT IS SOLVING A DIFFERENT PROBLEM
================================================================================

Spot Trader

Objective

    • Acquire or dispose of an asset.

------------------------------------------------------------

Derivatives Trader

Objective

    • Profit from future price movement.

------------------------------------------------------------

Market Maker

Objective

    • Continuously provide liquidity.
    • Earn the bid-ask spread.
    • Manage inventory risk.

------------------------------------------------------------

Institution

Objective

    • Execute very large orders.
    • Minimize market impact.
    • Obtain favorable execution prices.

------------------------------------------------------------

High Frequency Trader (HFT)

Objective

    • Exploit very short-lived opportunities.
    • React faster than competitors.

------------------------------------------------------------

Arbitrageur

Objective

    • Profit from temporary price differences
      between markets.

================================================================================
IMPORTANT OBSERVATION
================================================================================

Every participant may observe

    The same market.

But each participant asks

    A different question.

Because their objectives differ,

their decisions differ.

Because their decisions differ,

their orders differ.

Because their orders interact,

market behavior emerges.

================================================================================
THE MARKET AS A COMPLEX SYSTEM
================================================================================

Think of the market as a living ecosystem.

Every participant continuously

      Observe

        ↓

      Think

        ↓

      Decide

        ↓

      Submit Orders

        ↓

      Affect Liquidity

        ↓

      Affect Other Participants

        ↓

      Repeat

The market is therefore

    A network of interacting decision makers.
