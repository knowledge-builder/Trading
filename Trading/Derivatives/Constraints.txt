================================================================================
MARKET CONSTRAINT PHILOSOPHY
================================================================================

Objective

    Understand the constraints acting on price.

NOT

    Predict price with certainty.

================================================================================
WHY CONSTRAINTS?
================================================================================

Price does not move freely.

Price moves within constraints created by

    • Liquidity

    • Order Book

    • Market Participants

    • Capital

    • Time

    • Market Rules

These constraints influence

    • Direction

    • Speed

    • Magnitude

    • Probability

================================================================================
EXAMPLE
================================================================================

Current Order Book

    Buy Liquidity

        5,000 contracts

    Sell Liquidity

        500 contracts

Observation

    Buy side possesses significantly more
    available liquidity.

Possible Interpretation

    • Upward movement becomes easier.

    • Downward movement requires greater
      selling pressure.

Notice

    This does NOT guarantee
    price will increase.

It only changes the constraints
under which price can move.

================================================================================
SCIENTIFIC VIEW
================================================================================

We are NOT predicting

    Future Price

We ARE estimating

    • Current market constraints.

    • Possible outcomes.

    • Relative probabilities.
