we study them as an interacting ecosystem.

================================================================================
NOTICE SOMETHING IMPORTANT
================================================================================

Nobody directly changes price.

Every participant only submits orders.

Those orders interact through

    • Liquidity

    • Matching Engine

Price is therefore

    An emergent consequence,

not a decision made by any participant.

================================================================================
PARTICIPANT #1
SPOT TRADERS
================================================================================

Definition

People or organizations that buy or sell
the actual asset.

Objective

    Own the asset.

Examples

    Buy Bitcoin

    Hold Ethereum

Typical Decisions

    Buy

    Hold

    Sell

Observable Inputs

    • Price

    • Trend

    • Volume

Produces

    Buy Orders

    Sell Orders

Influences

    Liquidity

    Price

================================================================================
PARTICIPANT #2
DERIVATIVES TRADERS
================================================================================

Definition

Participants trading contracts whose value
depends on the underlying asset.

Objective

    Profit from future price movement.

They care about

    Direction.

Not ownership.

Typical Decisions

    Open Long

    Open Short

    Close Position

Observable Inputs

    • Price

    • Volume

    • Funding

    • Open Interest

Produces

    Long Orders

    Short Orders

Influences

    Liquidity

    Price Discovery

================================================================================
PARTICIPANT #3
MARKET MAKERS
================================================================================

Definition

Participants continuously providing
buy and sell quotes.

Objective

    Earn the bid-ask spread.

Secondary Objective

    Avoid excessive inventory.

Typical Decisions

    Add Bid

    Add Ask

    Adjust Quotes

Observable Inputs

    • Order Book

    • Spread

    • Inventory

Produces

    Liquidity

Without market makers

Liquidity would become thin.

================================================================================
PARTICIPANT #4
INSTITUTIONS
================================================================================

Definition

Large financial organizations.

Examples

    Asset Managers

    Hedge Funds

    Pension Funds

    Banks

Objective

    Execute very large positions
    efficiently.

Typical Decisions

    Split Orders

    Hide Intent

    Minimize Market Impact

Observable Inputs

    • Macro Events

    • Valuation

    • Risk Models

Produces

    Sustained Order Flow

Often responsible for

    Large trends.

================================================================================
PARTICIPANT #5
HIGH FREQUENCY TRADERS (HFT)
================================================================================

Definition

Algorithms operating at extremely
high speed.

Objective

    Exploit tiny opportunities.

Typical Decisions

    Submit Orders

    Cancel Orders

    Update Quotes

Observable Inputs

    • Order Book

    • Latency

    • Market Microstructure

Produces

    Extremely rapid liquidity changes.

================================================================================
PARTICIPANT #6
ARBITRAGEURS
================================================================================

Definition

Participants exploiting temporary
price differences.

Example

Binance

    BTC = $120,000

Bybit

    BTC = $120,050

Decision

    Buy Binance

    Sell Bybit

Result

Prices converge.

Objective

    Keep markets aligned.

Produces

    Cross-market efficiency.

================================================================================
PARTICIPANT #7
RETAIL TRADERS
================================================================================

Definition

Individual traders.

Important Clarification

Retail is NOT another market mechanism.

Retail describes

    The size and nature of the participant.

A retail trader may be

    • Spot Trader

    • Derivatives Trader

    • Swing Trader

    • Scalper

Retail simply means

    An individual participant.

================================================================================
THE MARKET IS A FEEDBACK SYSTEM
================================================================================

Participant A

    submits an order.

            │

            ▼

Price changes.

            │

            ▼

Participant B observes
the new price.

            │

            ▼

Participant B submits
another order.

            │

            ▼

Participant C reacts.

            │

            ▼

Price changes again.

No participant acts in isolation.

Each participant continuously reacts
to the actions of others.

================================================================================
FIRST-PRINCIPLES INSIGHT
================================================================================

The market is NOT simply

    Buyers

versus

    Sellers.

The market is a complex adaptive system
composed of many participant classes,
each with different objectives,
constraints,
information,
and decision rules.

Their continuous interaction produces

    • Order Flow

    • Liquidity Changes

    • Price Discovery

    • Trends

    • Volatility

Understanding the market therefore requires
understanding the behavior and interaction
of its participants rather than studying
price alone.
