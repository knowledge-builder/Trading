================================================================================
MARKET LEARNING FRAMEWORK
Physics of Financial Markets (Derivatives-First)
================================================================================

Author's Note
-------------
This documentation establishes the default assumptions for all future
discussions regarding trading, market microstructure, liquidity, and price
movement.

The objective is to build a FIRST-PRINCIPLES understanding of financial markets
from the perspective of a derivatives trader.

This is NOT a strategy guide.

This is an attempt to understand the underlying mechanisms that govern
financial markets.

================================================================================
DEFAULT ASSUMPTION
================================================================================

Unless explicitly stated otherwise,

ALL discussions assume we are operating in:

    • Crypto Perpetual Futures
    • Futures Markets
    • Derivatives Markets

NOT

    • Spot Trading
    • Traditional Investing
    • Long-term Asset Ownership

Reason

    • This matches the actual environment in which I trade.

    • Learning should begin from the domain where knowledge
      will actually be applied.

================================================================================
TERMINOLOGY
================================================================================

Avoid interpreting the platform literally.

Instead mentally translate the buttons.

Platform Button

    BUY

Interpret As

    Open Long Position

------------------------------------------------------------

Platform Button

    SELL

Interpret As

    Open Short Position

Reason

    • In derivatives, BUY does not necessarily mean
      acquiring ownership of the asset.

    • Likewise, SELL does not necessarily mean
      selling an asset already owned.

They represent directional positions.

================================================================================
CORE PHILOSOPHY
================================================================================

This learning process focuses on MECHANISMS.

Rather than asking

    • How do I make money?

Ask

    • What mechanism makes profit possible?

------------------------------------------------------------

Rather than asking

    • When should I buy?

Ask

    • What actually happens when I open a Long position?

------------------------------------------------------------

Rather than asking

    • Why did price move?

Ask

    • What interaction caused price to move?

The objective is explanation before prediction.

================================================================================
FIRST-PRINCIPLES APPROACH
================================================================================

Every concept should be reduced into four fundamental questions.

------------------------------------------------------------

1. COMPONENTS

Questions

    • What objects exist?

    • Who participates?

    • What are the building blocks?

Examples

    • Traders

    • Orders

    • Positions

    • Liquidity

    • Matching Engine

------------------------------------------------------------

2. INTERACTIONS

Questions

    • How do these components interact?

    • What causes one component to affect another?

Examples

    • Market Orders consume liquidity.

    • Limit Orders provide liquidity.

    • Buyers and sellers are matched.

------------------------------------------------------------

3. CONSTRAINTS

Questions

    • What MUST happen?

    • What CANNOT happen?

Examples

    • Every executed trade requires counterparties.

    • Liquidity cannot become negative.

    • Orders cannot execute without available liquidity.

------------------------------------------------------------

4. EMERGENT BEHAVIOR

Questions

    • What larger behavior emerges?

    • Why does it naturally arise?

Examples

    • Trends

    • Reversals

    • Breakouts

    • Volatility

LEARNING STYLE
================================================================================

Every topic should be introduced in the following order.

1. Big Picture

    • Why does this concept exist?

------------------------------------------------------------

2. Components

    • What objects are involved?

------------------------------------------------------------

3. Mechanism

    • How do those objects interact?

------------------------------------------------------------

4. Constraints

    • What must happen?

    • What cannot happen?

------------------------------------------------------------

5. Thought Experiments

    • Simulate scenarios.

    • Build intuition.

------------------------------------------------------------

6. Mathematics

    • Quantify the mechanism.

------------------------------------------------------------

7. Statistics

    • Validate observations.

------------------------------------------------------------

8. Applications

    • Trading

    • Market Analysis

    • Risk Management

================================================================================
FINAL OBJECTIVE
================================================================================

The goal is NOT to memorize trading concepts.

The goal is to develop a mechanistic intuition.

An intuition where every market event is interpreted through
the interaction of underlying components.

Ultimately, every explanation should answer:

    • What happened?

    • Why did it happen?

    • Which mechanism caused it?

    • Which constraints governed it?

Only after understanding these questions should mathematics,
statistics, and trading strategy be introduced.
