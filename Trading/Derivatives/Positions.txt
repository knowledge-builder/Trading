
================================================================================
LESSON 1 — POSITIONS
Understanding Financial Exposure
================================================================================

Objective
---------
Develop an intuitive understanding of what a POSITION is before learning
Long, Short, Profit & Loss, Leverage, and Liquidity.

This lesson intentionally avoids mathematics and focuses on the underlying
mechanism.

================================================================================
COMMON MISCONCEPTION
================================================================================

A Position is NOT:

    • Bitcoin
    • Ethereum
    • Cash
    • A completed trade
    • Profit
    • Loss

A position is something entirely different.

================================================================================
DEFINITION
================================================================================

A Position is:

    "Your current financial exposure to future market price movement."

Breaking the definition down

Financial

    • The position can gain value.
    • The position can lose value.

Exposure

    • Your account is now affected by market movement.

Future Price Movement

    • Every price change affects the value
      of your position while it remains open.

================================================================================
MENTAL MODEL
================================================================================

Without a Position

        Market Moves
              │
              ▼
      Nothing Happens To You

------------------------------------------------------------

With a Position

        Market Moves
              │
              ▼
    Position Changes In Value

The market moves regardless of whether you participate.

A position simply determines whether your account is affected.

================================================================================
THE IMPORTANT SHIFT
================================================================================

Do NOT think

    "I own Ethereum."

Instead think

    "I have exposure to Ethereum's price movement."

In derivatives,

the position is the primary object,

not the asset itself.

================================================================================
ANALOGY — WEATHER
================================================================================

Suppose tomorrow it rains.

Does everyone benefit?

No.

Examples

Farmer

    Rain
        ↓
    Good

Outdoor Wedding

    Rain
        ↓
    Bad

Same event.

Different outcome.

Reason

    • They occupy different positions.

Markets work exactly the same way.

The same price movement affects traders differently
depending on the position they hold.

================================================================================
THOUGHT EXPERIMENT #1
================================================================================

Current Market

    ETH = $2,000

Three traders observe the exact same market.

------------------------------------------------------------

Alice

    • No Position

------------------------------------------------------------

Bob

    • Long Position

------------------------------------------------------------

Carol

    • Short Position

================================================================================
MARKET MOVES
================================================================================

ETH increases

    $2,000

        ↓

    $2,100

Question

What happened?

Answer

Alice

    • No financial change.

    • She had no market exposure.

------------------------------------------------------------

Bob

    • Position increased in value.

    • The market moved in his favor.

------------------------------------------------------------

Carol

    • Position decreased in value.

    • The market moved against her.

Notice

    • The market moved only once.

    • Three different financial outcomes occurred.

Reason

    • Their positions were different.

================================================================================
IMPORTANT DISTINCTION
================================================================================

Avoid saying

    • Bob won.

    • Carol lost.

Instead say

    • Bob's position increased in value.

    • Carol's position decreased in value.

Reason

    • Neither trader has closed the position.

    • Profit or loss has not yet been realized.

================================================================================
OPEN POSITION
================================================================================

Suppose neither Bob nor Carol closes the position.

Market

    $2,000

        ↓

    $2,100

        ↓

    $2,300

        ↓

    $1,950

        ↓

    $2,400

Question

Can we already say Bob has made money?

Answer

Not yet.

His position has gained value,

but that value continues changing as long as
the position remains open.

================================================================================
POSITION LIFE CYCLE
================================================================================

	Open Position

			│

			▼

	Market Continues Moving

			│

			▼

	Position Value Continuously Changes

			│

			▼

	Close Position

			│

			▼

	Profit / Loss Becomes Fixed

================================================================================
ANALOGY — TAKING A PHOTOGRAPH
================================================================================

Imagine recording a moving object.

Before taking the picture

    • Everything continues moving.

After taking the picture

    • One moment becomes permanently captured.

Closing a position behaves similarly.

While open

    • Position value constantly changes.

Once closed

    • The outcome becomes fixed.

================================================================================
FOUNDATIONAL PRINCIPLE
================================================================================

The market continuously reprices every OPEN position.

Only a CLOSED position has a fixed outcome.

------------------------------------------------------------

Open Position

    • Position value changes continuously.

    • Profit or loss is NOT yet finalized.

------------------------------------------------------------

Closed Position

    • Position value stops changing.

    • Profit or loss becomes realized.

================================================================================
FIRST-PRINCIPLES INSIGHT
================================================================================

The market does not reward or punish traders.

The market simply moves.

Your POSITION determines how that movement
affects your account.

================================================================================
KEY TAKEAWAYS
================================================================================

• A position represents financial exposure,
  not asset ownership.

• The market moves independently of you.

• An open position continuously changes in value.

• Closing a position freezes the final outcome.

• The same market movement can produce different
  results for different traders because they hold
  different positions.

• Before learning Long and Short,
  it is essential to understand that
  a position is simply your relationship
  to future market price movement.
