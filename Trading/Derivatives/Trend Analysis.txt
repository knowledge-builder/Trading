
• HH (Higher High) – A new high that is higher than the previous high. Indicates buyers are pushing price higher.
• LH (Lower High) – A new high that is lower than the previous high. Indicates buyers are becoming weaker.
• HL (Higher Low) – A new low that is higher than the previous low. Indicates buyers are defending higher prices.
• LL (Lower Low) – A new low that is lower than the previous low. Indicates sellers are pushing price lower.


• HH = Buyers made a higher peak.
• HL = Buyers defended a higher floor.
• LH = Buyers failed to reach the previous peak.
• LL = Sellers pushed to a lower floor.


===========================================================================

Key Principle

HH, HL, LH, and LL are NOT determined by candle color.

They are determined by price action—specifically the High and Low reached by price.


Candle Color
------------

	• A candle can be:

		• 🟢 Green
		• 🔴 Red
		• Doji

	• Any of them can form a HH, HL, LH, or LL.

	• Candle color does not define market structure.
	
	

Psychology
-----------
	• While candle color does not determine market structure, it reveals the psychology behind it.

		Scenario A
		----------
			• Long green candle

				████████
			• Strong buyers.


		Scenario B
		----------
			• Tiny doji

				──┼──
			• Indecision.


		Scenario C
		----------
			• Long upper wick

					 │

				 ████

					 │
			• Buyers pushed price higher...

			• but sellers forced it back down before the candle closed.

			• That upper wick tells a story that market structure alone doesn't.
	
	
===========================================================================

Mental Model

	• Market Structure answers: "Where did price go?"
	• Candlestick Psychology answers: "How did price get there?"
	
	
===========================================================================

Practice

For every swing, ask yourself:

	• Where is the latest swing high?
	• Where is the latest swing low?
	• Did buyers create a Higher High?
	• Did buyers defend a Higher Low?
	• Did sellers create a Lower Low?
	• Did buyers fail to create a Higher High?
	• What story do the candles tell at those swing points?
