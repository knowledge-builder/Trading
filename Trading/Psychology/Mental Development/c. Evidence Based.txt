===============================================================================
FROM PERSUASION TO VALIDATION
Mental Conditioning for Evidence-Based Trading
===============================================================================

Core Principle
--------------

Never confuse credibility with evidence.

Never confuse transparency with effectiveness.

Never confuse persuasion with validation.

===============================================================================
The Psychological Bias
===============================================================================

The brain naturally performs the following shortcut.

    Transparent Trader

            ↓

    Honest Person

            ↓

    Successful Trader

            ↓

    Effective Strategy

The first conclusion may be reasonable.

The last conclusion has not yet been proven.

===============================================================================
The Scientific Mindset
===============================================================================

When observing another trader, think:

    "This is not proof."

    "This is a hypothesis."

The trader becomes the source of an idea to investigate,
not the source of truth.

===============================================================================
Research Process
===============================================================================

    Observe

        ↓

    Form Hypothesis

        ↓

    Collect Data

        ↓

    Backtest

        ↓

    Forward Test

        ↓

    Measure Results

        ↓

    Draw Conclusions

===============================================================================
Daily Mental Conditioning
===============================================================================

Before adopting any strategy, ask:

    □ What evidence have I personally verified?

    □ What assumptions am I making?

    □ Am I persuaded by results, or by the process that produced them?

    □ If this strategy came from an unknown person, would I still
      evaluate it the same way?

===============================================================================
Final Principle
===============================================================================

A convincing demonstration is not the end of research.

It is the beginning of research.

The disciplined trader does not inherit conviction.

They build conviction through evidence.
===============================================================================
