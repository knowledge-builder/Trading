===============================================================================
THE TESTING MINDSET
From Assumptions to Evidence
===============================================================================

Objective
---------

Develop the habit of treating every observation, intuition, strategy,
or idea as a hypothesis that requires validation.

The objective is not to prove yourself right.

The objective is to discover what is actually true.

===============================================================================
The Core Principle
===============================================================================

Do not immediately conclude.

Instead,

Observe.

Question.

Test.

Measure.

Then conclude.

===============================================================================
A Refined Scientific Principle
===============================================================================

Instead of saying

    "Assume nothing. Question everything."

Adopt a more practical mindset.

    "Assume as little as possible.

     Validate as much as possible."

Every assumption should eventually become either

    • Supported by evidence

or

    • Rejected by evidence.

===============================================================================
The Scientific Thinking Process
===============================================================================

                  Observation

                      ↓

                   Question

                      ↓

                 Hypothesis

                      ↓

               Design Experiment

                      ↓

               Collect Evidence

                      ↓

                 Analyze Data

                      ↓

                 Draw Conclusion

                      ↓

               Update Beliefs

                      ↓

                    Repeat

Scientific thinking is iterative.

Every conclusion remains open to revision when new evidence becomes
available.

===============================================================================
The Common Cognitive Mistake
===============================================================================

Many people unconsciously follow this process.

           Observation

                ↓

        Immediate Conclusion

                ↓

         Confirmation Bias

                ↓

         Reinforce Belief

This process feels natural.

However,

It often produces conclusions based more on intuition than evidence.

===============================================================================
The Testing Mindset
===============================================================================

A disciplined researcher inserts an important step.

            Observation

                ↓

            Hypothesis

                ↓

             Testing

                ↓

             Evidence

                ↓

           Conclusion

The hypothesis delays certainty.

The experiment determines whether the hypothesis deserves confidence.

===============================================================================
Trading Example
===============================================================================

Observation

    Large buying pressure appears in the order book.

Emotional Conclusion

    "Price is going to increase."

Testing Mindset

    Hypothesis

        Large buying pressure may increase the probability
        of short-term upward price movement.

Research Questions

    • How often does this happen?

    • Under what market conditions?

    • How long does the effect last?

    • Does liquidity size matter?

    • Does this remain true across different assets?

Only after testing should a conclusion be formed.

===============================================================================
Strategy Evaluation Example
===============================================================================

Observation

    A trader consistently profits while trading live.

Emotional Conclusion

    "His strategy works."

Testing Mindset

    Hypothesis

        This strategy may have positive expectancy.

Research Process

    • Identify the trading rules.

    • Collect sufficient trade data.

    • Backtest the strategy.

    • Forward test the strategy.

    • Measure performance.

Only then should confidence increase.

===============================================================================
Feature Engineering Example
===============================================================================

Observation

    A new predictor variable has been created.

Example

    Order Book Imbalance

Emotional Conclusion

    "This will improve my model."

Testing Mindset

    Hypothesis

        Order Book Imbalance may improve predictive performance.

Research Process

    • Add the feature.

    • Train the model.

    • Compare against the baseline.

    • Evaluate performance metrics.

    • Determine whether the improvement is statistically meaningful.

===============================================================================
The Goal Is Not To Be Right
===============================================================================

An immature mindset asks

    "How can I prove my idea is correct?"

A scientific mindset asks

    "How can I discover whether my idea is correct?"

These are fundamentally different objectives.

===============================================================================
Belief Formation
===============================================================================

Beliefs should not originate from

    • Excitement

    • Intuition

    • Authority

    • Popularity

    • Marketing

Beliefs should emerge from

    • Observation

    • Evidence

    • Repeated testing

    • Statistical analysis

    • Reproducible results

===============================================================================
Mental Conditioning
===============================================================================

Whenever an idea appears, ask

    □ What exactly did I observe?

    □ What assumptions am I making?

    □ What evidence currently supports this idea?

    □ What evidence would contradict this idea?

    □ How can I test this objectively?

    □ What data do I need before reaching a conclusion?

These questions slow down emotional reasoning and encourage
analytical thinking.

===============================================================================
Daily Mental Conditioning Statements
===============================================================================

    • Every idea begins as a hypothesis.

    • Evidence determines confidence.

    • I delay conclusions until sufficient data is available.

    • Being wrong is part of learning.

    • Every failed hypothesis improves my understanding.

    • My objective is not to defend my ideas.

    • My objective is to understand reality.

===============================================================================
Final Principle
===============================================================================

A researcher is not measured by how many hypotheses are confirmed.

A researcher is measured by the willingness to let evidence
change previously held beliefs.

Scientific maturity is the ability to say

    "This idea seemed promising.

     The evidence says otherwise.

     Therefore, I will revise my understanding."

The goal is not certainty.

The goal is continuously improving the quality of your understanding
through observation, testing, and evidence.
