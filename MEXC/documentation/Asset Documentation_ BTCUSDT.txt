# Asset Documentation: BTCUSDT

## Overview

BTCUSDT is a cryptocurrency trading pair representing the exchange rate between:

    BTC  = Bitcoin
    USDT = Tether (USD-pegged stablecoin)

The pair expresses the value of Bitcoin in terms of USDT.

Example:

    BTCUSDT = 65,927.75

Means:

    1 BTC = 65,927.75 USDT

In practical terms:

    Buying BTCUSDT
        =
    Buying Bitcoin using USDT

    Selling BTCUSDT
        =
    Selling Bitcoin and receiving USDT

# Understanding Trading Pairs

A trading pair always has two components:


        BASE / QUOTE


Example:

        BTC / USDT


## Base Asset

The first symbol:

    BTC


Represents the asset being traded.


Meaning:

    "How much BTC are we buying or selling?"


Example:

    Buy 1 BTC


You are acquiring:

    1 Bitcoin



---

## Quote Asset

The second symbol:

    USDT


Represents the currency used to price the base asset.


Meaning:

    "How much does BTC cost?"


Example:

    BTCUSDT = 65,927


Means:

    1 BTC costs 65,927 USDT

# What is Bitcoin (BTC)?

## Definition

Bitcoin is a decentralized digital currency created in 2009.

It operates on:

    Blockchain technology


Unlike traditional currencies:

    USD
    EUR
    PHP


Bitcoin is not controlled by a central bank.


---

# Bitcoin Characteristics

## 1. Fixed Supply

Bitcoin has a maximum supply:

    21 million BTC


This creates scarcity.

Comparison:

Gold:

    Limited physical supply

Bitcoin:

    Limited digital supply



---

## 2. Decentralized Network

Bitcoin transactions are validated by:

    Global network of computers


No single entity controls:

    - issuance
    - transaction validation
    - monetary policy


---

## 3. Blockchain Ledger

Every Bitcoin transaction is recorded on:

    Bitcoin blockchain


Structure:

    Transaction
        |
        ▼
    Block
        |
        ▼
    Blockchain

# What is USDT?

## Definition

USDT (Tether) is a stablecoin designed to maintain:

    1 USDT ≈ 1 USD


It is commonly used as a digital representation of USD inside cryptocurrency exchanges.


---
